# Lenny Rachitsky — takeaways from Netflix CPTO Elizabeth Stone

Eight-point thread summarizing a podcast conversation with Netflix CPTO Elizabeth Stone on systems thinking as the top AI-era skill, org-culture convergence, the keeper's test, and AI fluency.

## Key claims extracted

- Systems thinking is the most important AI-era skill; learnable by zooming out one level from the specific problem
- Current role confusion is a predictable "storming before forming" phase
- Top AI labs converged on traits (high agency, high talent density) already present in Netflix's early culture
- Keeper's test used mainly for positive performance conversations, not firing
- Netflix adds one cross-level "AI fluency" aspiration rather than rewriting career ladders per level
