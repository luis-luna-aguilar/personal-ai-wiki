# Claude Science beta

Anthropic's Claude Science product page describes a public beta app for scientific analysis, database search, reproducible artifacts, scientific connectors, and local/HPC compute integration.

## Influenced pages

- [Claude Science](../../tools/claude-science.md) - product page
- [State of Science](../../state-of/science.md) - science agent platform entry
- [AI in Science](../../trends/ai-in-science.md) - workflow-platform and verification trend

## Key claims extracted

- Claude Science is an app, not a new model.
- It runs analyses, searches databases, and traces each step from data wrangling to publication.
- Figures, tables, and notebooks include the code, environment, and conversation that produced them.
- It supports persistent Python and R kernels, local/HPC execution, scientific connectors, and 60+ scientific databases.
