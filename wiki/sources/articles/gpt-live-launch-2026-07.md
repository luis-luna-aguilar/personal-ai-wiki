# Introducing GPT-Live

OpenAI announced GPT-Live, a full-duplex voice model family that powers the new ChatGPT Voice experience. GPT-Live continuously listens and speaks, manages conversational timing, and delegates search, reasoning, or more agentic work to frontier models such as GPT-5.5 in the background.

## Key claims extracted

- GPT-Live-1 and GPT-Live-1 mini are rolling out globally in ChatGPT Voice.
- GPT-Live-1 is default for Go, Plus, and Pro users; GPT-Live-1 mini is default for Free users.
- GPT-Live is full-duplex: it can listen and speak at the same time.
- For deeper work, GPT-Live delegates to GPT-5.5 at launch and can keep the conversation flowing while the task runs.
- API access is planned soon but not yet live at launch.
- OpenAI reports strong human preference over Advanced Voice Mode and gains on GPQA, BrowseComp, and an internal τ³-Voice Telecom variant.
- ChatGPT Voice does not support voice with video or screen sharing at launch.
