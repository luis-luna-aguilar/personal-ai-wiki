# BleepingComputer — OpenAI launches GPT-6.1 Terra

Mayank Parmar's same-day report on GPT-6.1 Terra, quoting OpenAI's blog post: a fast, efficient model for computer use, visual reasoning, coding and everyday work, priced well below OpenAI's larger models.

## Key claims extracted
- Launched 2026-10-26; API `gpt-6.1-terra`; Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu in ChatGPT Work and Codex
- $1/$5 per 1M input/output tokens, 50% lower than GPT-5.6 Terra and GPT-6.1 Sol
- Agents' Last Exam: +15 points over GPT-5.6 Terra at medium effort at about a third of the cost; OpenAI says on par with GPT-6 Astra at a tenth of its per-token price
- More than 3x faster than GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol
- Internal error rate: 16.7% at low effort (GPT-5.6 Terra 53.6%); 4.3% at max effort, down 18 points
- Terminal-Bench 4.0: 50.3% at max effort (GPT-5.6 Terra 21.5%)
- OpenAI still recommends GPT-6.1 Sol or GPT-6 Astra for the most complex coding
