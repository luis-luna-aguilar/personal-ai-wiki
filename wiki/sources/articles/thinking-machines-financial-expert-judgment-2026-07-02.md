# Learning to Replicate Expert Judgment in Financial Tasks

Thinking Machines describes a Bridgewater AIA Labs collaboration on training models to replicate expert investor judgment for six financial information-filtering tasks.

## Influenced pages

- [Cost-aware AI task routing](../../training/cost-aware-ai-task-routing.md) - verified domain-tuned model case
- [State of Finance](../../state-of/finance.md) - institutional finance AI signal

## Key claims extracted

- Frontier models with naive prompts averaged roughly 50% accuracy on the task set.
- Expert-written prompts raised performance to the mid-70s but still below Bridgewater's daily-work threshold.
- Qwen3-235B with the final training recipe reached 84.66% average accuracy and 92.99 average positive-class F1.
- Thinking Machines reports the trained model improved average accuracy from 78.2% to 84.7%, made 29.8% fewer mistakes than the best frontier model evaluated, and reduced inference cost per task by 13.8x.
