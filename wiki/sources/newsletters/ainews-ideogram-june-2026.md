# AINews — Ideogram 4, Reve 2 (June)

AINews roundup focused on the layout breakthrough in image generation. Ideogram 4.0 released as open weights (#8 overall Image Arena, #1 open image model; 9.3B DiT; fp8/nf4 checkpoints, ComfyUI support, strong text rendering and branding). Also covered: MS MAI-Thinking-1 (109-page tech report, zero synthetic data/distillation), Gemma 4 12B (encoder-free multimodal), open audio models (Miso One, Fun-Realtime-TTS), agent harness shifts, routing debate, and enterprise cost controls.
