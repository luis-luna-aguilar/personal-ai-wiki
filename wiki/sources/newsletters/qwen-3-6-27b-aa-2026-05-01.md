# Qwen 3.6 27B Artificial Analysis follow-up

AINews summarizes Artificial Analysis as ranking Qwen 3.6 27B the leading open-weight model under 150B parameters, while flagging unusually expensive output-token usage on the evaluation suite.

## Key claims extracted

- Intelligence Index score reported as 46 for Qwen 3.6 27B.
- Reported attributes include Apache 2.0 licensing, 262K context, native multimodal input, and BF16 weights small enough for one H100.
- Cost caveat: AINews says AA estimated about 144M output tokens and roughly 21× Gemma 4 31B's cost on the suite.
