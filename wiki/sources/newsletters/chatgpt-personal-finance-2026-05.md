# ChatGPT becomes your personal CFO — newsletter coverage

Newsletter coverage of OpenAI's @Finances personal finance agent launch inside ChatGPT. Connects to 12,000+ financial providers and gives personalized money advice grounded in the user's real income and spending data. Available via `@Finances` in any ChatGPT conversation; US Pro preview only. Benchmark scores: GPT-5.5 Thinking 79/100, GPT-5.5 Pro 82.5/100 on complex personal-finance tasks. Launch video hit 14M+ views in 48 hours. Newsletter also covers the Claude Mythos / Apple M5 story.
