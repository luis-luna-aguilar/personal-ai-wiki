# Meet the autonomous growth agent (Superhuman)

Superhuman's 2026-05-21 newsletter (Zain Kahn), covering two separate stories used by different proposals. The lead story is Tempo (withtempo.ai) launching an AI "head of growth": an always-on, unprompted agent that builds and deploys a weekly growth plan by pulling from a company's ad accounts, reviews, and e-commerce platform. It is one of seven agent roles on the platform, each with decisions and train of thought tracked live on a canvas; Superhuman puts the launch video at 1.5M+ views. This is a single secondary-newsletter blurb: no Tempo product page, case study, or independent coverage was fetched. Item 3 of the same issue covers OpenAI's Guaranteed Capacity announcement, extracted separately below.
