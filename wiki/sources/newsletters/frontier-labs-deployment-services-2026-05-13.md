# Frontier labs as deployment-service firms

Several May 2026 newsletter sources frame OpenAI and Anthropic as moving from model/API vendors toward deployment-service firms. The common claim is that enterprise value is bottlenecked by context, permissions, evals, workflow design, and human handoffs.

## Influenced pages

- [Agents reshape organizations](../../trends/agents-reshape-organizations.md)
- [State of Agents](../../state-of/agents.md)

## Key claims extracted

- Frontier labs are packaging more of the deployment layer around models.
- Workflow redesign and integration appear more important than raw model access for enterprise adoption.
- Vertical templates and services are becoming a competitive surface.
