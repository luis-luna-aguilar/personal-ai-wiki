# Every — Skip: GPT-6.1 Terra (Context Window, 2026-10-29)

The "Skip" item in Every's 2026-10-29 Context Window newsletter (Grant Harvey). Every's team declined to run a full Vibe Check on GPT-6.1 Terra and recommended Gemini 4 Argon for editing instead. This page covers only the Terra item; the newsletter's other stories are separate.

## Key claims extracted
- Terra is priced at half GPT-6.1 Sol and pitched for computer use
- "It's fast at simple tasks like installing Google Chrome, but fails on more complex tasks like setting up this article in Every's content management system"; the same item later says "No one here has tested its computer use yet"
- OpenAI says it is "significantly better at writing"; Dan Shipper: "We ran it but didn't see enough of a difference to [run a] Vibe Check"
- Harvey's test: the first newsletter draft was full of AI tells; on the second and third tries it took notes well and edited better than previous GPT models — "a capable iterative writer that still needs a lot of human steering"
