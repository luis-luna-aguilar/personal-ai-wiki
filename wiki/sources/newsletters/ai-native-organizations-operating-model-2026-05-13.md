# AI-native organizations as operating model

The May 2026 digest clusters several organization-design signals: agent-only startup rhetoric, humans as expensive models, agent architect roles, Coinbase-style AI-native expectations, and a split between delegation and close collaboration.

## Influenced pages

- [Agents reshape organizations](../../trends/agents-reshape-organizations.md)
- [Company-wide AI enablement](../../training/company-wide-ai-enablement.md)

## Key claims extracted

- The strongest reusable concept is not "agents replace teams," but that humans allocate attention, define boundaries, and review outputs.
- AI-native adoption requires operating-design changes, not only tool access.
- Delegation and collaboration modes need to be taught explicitly.
