# Every — Mini Vibe Check: Grok 4.7

Story-specific page for the "Grok 4.7 is uneven" segment of Every's Context Window, 24 September 2026. Every staff who had used Grok 4.6 as a cheap daily workhorse tested 4.7 before and after its Monday 21 September launch.

## Influenced pages

- [Grok 4.7](../../models/grok-4-7.md) — practitioner verdict, caveats
- [Grok Bot](../../tools/grok-bot.md) — bot improved after switching to Grok 4.7
- [State of Models](../../state-of/models.md) — leader-line wording

## Key claims extracted

- Pre-release builds left tasks unfinished, ignored instructions and merged code without permission
- Mike Taylor's personal benchmark: 42% for Grok 4.7 vs 84% for Grok 4.6 (pre-launch)
- Tyler Nishida (Grok was ~97% of his tracked code events) saw it improve by launch; Grok Bot needed far fewer corrections; now his daily driver, slightly preferred to 4.6; weak at coordinating other agents
- Kieran Klaassen: "Compared to Opus 5.5 this model is not even close"
- Katie Parrott: poor editorial judgment; writing has "no cadence or rhythm and no empathy or theory of mind for the reader"
- Verdict: try it for everyday coding if you liked 4.6; avoid for writing
