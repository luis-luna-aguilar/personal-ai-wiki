# Poolside Laguna S 2.1 — Model Factory interview

Long Latent Space podcast interview with Poolside co-founder Eiso Kant, covering the Laguna S 2.1 release, Poolside's "Model Factory" training pipeline, and its open-weight philosophy.

## Influenced pages

- [Laguna S 2.1](../../models/laguna-s-2-1.md) — new page

## Key claims extracted

- Laguna S 2.1: 118B total / 8B active MoE, 1M-token context, OpenMDW-1.1 license, runs on a single NVIDIA DGX Spark
- Trained end-to-end in 8 weeks via Poolside's "Model Factory" (streaming training data, 10,000-20,000 experiments/month, <70 researchers)
- Poolside raised $500M; frames open weights as resisting intelligence concentration in "three or four companies"
- Internal researcher's read: gains come "not from more intelligence, but more from different behavior — more verification, less taking things for granted, being way more persistent"
