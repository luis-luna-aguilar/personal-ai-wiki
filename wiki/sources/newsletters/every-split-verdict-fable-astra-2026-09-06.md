# A Split Verdict on Fable vs. Astra

Every's weekly digest recapping a subscriber-only comparison camp: staff split on which model is the better daily driver, but converged that Fable 5.1 needed fewer clicks, got a diagram right that Astra decorated with nonsensical flourishes, and won a head-to-head essay draft. Also links Every's Astra review (71/100 Senior Engineer Bench, up from 56 for GPT-5.6 Sol) and Fable 5.1 review (matched Opus 5's agent results in ~60% time/half tokens, but overshot a prompted quote limit — 43 returned when 8-12 were asked for).
