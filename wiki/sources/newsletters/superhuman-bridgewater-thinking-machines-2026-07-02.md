# Superhuman - Bridgewater and Thinking Machines finance case

Superhuman summarizes a Bridgewater AIA Labs and Thinking Machines collaboration on teaching models to surface relevant financial news using expert prompts and fine-tuning.

## Influenced pages

- [Cost-aware AI task routing](../../training/cost-aware-ai-task-routing.md) - domain-tuned model routing case
- [State of Finance](../../state-of/finance.md) - finance workflow signal

## Key claims extracted

- Expert prompts lifted frontier-model accuracy from roughly 50% to the mid-70s on subjective financial relevance tasks.
- Fine-tuning Qwen3-235B on expert-labeled data improved reported accuracy from 78% to 85%.
- The newsletter reports roughly 14x lower cost per task versus leading frontier models.
