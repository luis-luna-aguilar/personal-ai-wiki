# Superhuman — Anthropic lands another high-profile hire (AI governance statement)

Superhuman's daily digest, whose main stories (AI demand/compute commentary, an Anthropic hire, TSMC earnings) are not actioned here — only the "AI pushback" item on economist-led governance demands is used.

## Influenced pages
- [AI governance and policy](../../trends/ai-governance-and-policy.md) — new trend page

## Key claims extracted
- Over 200 economists, including 16 Nobel laureates, signed "We Must Act Now," urging governments to steer AI development toward broad societal benefit
- A Verasight survey found 60% of respondents anxious about AI's rise
- 89% of survey respondents support requiring frontier labs to publicly disclose safety-testing results
- 81% want government authority to block potentially dangerous models before release
- 69% favor forcing AI firms to hand over equity stakes into a fund distributing AI's gains broadly, similar in spirit to OpenAI's own earlier proposal that the US government take a 5% stake in the company
- The US government has separately reviewed both Anthropic's and OpenAI's most powerful models prior to release
