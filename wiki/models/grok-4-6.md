# Grok 4.6

xAI's (SpaceXAI) successor to Grok 4.5, released 2026-08-13 as "a major step up from 4.5 at the same price." Still a 1.5T-parameter mixture-of-experts model, built via a longer supplemental training run, SFT trajectories regenerated by Grok 4.5 itself, and agentic RL across coding, web development, CAD, and kernel-optimization tasks. Powers the newly-launched Grok Bot AI-teammate product.

## Current status (as of 2026-08-13)

- Per Artificial Analysis: Intelligence Index 61 (near GPT-5.6 Sol Max, behind Claude Opus/Fable); 88.4% Terminal-Bench v2.1; GDPval-AA v2 Elo 1753; competitive AA-Briefcase performance at lower cost than peers
- Pricing: $2/M input, $6/M output — unchanged from Grok 4.5's launch pricing, keeping it the frontier's clearest price/performance leader
- Early Code Arena data slots it near GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Fable 5 on webdev tasks; practitioners (Pawel Huryn) framed it as the new default for coding/bug-finding workloads; available day-0 in Devin (Cognition)
- xAI reports more self-testing behavior during long agentic tasks versus 4.5; Elon Musk said Grok 4.7 is already in training, with supplemental training on SpaceX internal data planned
- Powers **Grok Bot**, launched the same day — see [Grok Bot](../tools/grok-bot.md)

## Why it matters

Supersedes [Grok 4.5](../history/models/grok-4-5.md) roughly a month after launch, holding the same $2/$6 pricing while closing much of the capability gap to the top tier — part of the same-day "Frontier Model Day" cluster (alongside Qwen3.8-Max open weights and DeepSeek V4 Pro GA) that AINews read as a pricing/capability compression signal at the top of the market.

## Weaknesses / caveats

- Still behind Claude Opus/Fable and roughly tied with GPT-5.6 Sol Max on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index — competing primarily on price/efficiency, not outright capability
- All figures are from Artificial Analysis via AINews' secondary reporting; no primary xAI benchmark disclosure beyond the training-process description

## Recent changes

- [2026-08-13] Launched: builds on Grok 4.5 with longer supplemental training, regenerated SFT trajectories, and broader agentic RL; AA Intelligence Index 61, 88.4% Terminal-Bench v2.1, unchanged $2/$6 pricing; powers new Grok Bot product
