# ChatGPT Space

OpenAI's ChatGPT-native home for documents, slides and spreadsheets that humans and agents edit together, launched at DevDay on 2026-09-29 alongside [ChatGPT dots](chatgpt-dots.md) and [ChatGPT Pages](chatgpt-pages.md). AINews frames Space, together with dots, as OpenAI's answer to Notion and the office productivity suite.

## Current status (as of 2026-10-04)

- Shared human/agent workspace for docs, slides and spreadsheets inside ChatGPT
- A user can tag their dot in a comment to hand it work on the document
- Every's Dan Shipper wrote his DevDay review in Space and reports that edits land faster than when an agent works in Google Docs
- Plugin extensions feed it: OpenAI's DevDay demo had calendar meeting notes flow into a Space, a feature OpenAI says is built on its Agents API
- Every counts dots and Space together as "the workspace itself" in OpenAI's plan to make ChatGPT the operating system for work

## Weaknesses / caveats

- Shipper expects that moving a team off Google Workspace or Notion will be the hard part, not the editing experience
- Week-one coverage is a few hands-on paragraphs; no detail yet on permissions, version history, export or pricing

## Recent changes

- [2026-10-04] Every names dots and Space as the core of OpenAI's "ChatGPT as operating system" plan.
- [2026-09-29] Launched at OpenAI DevDay with dots and Pages; dots can be tagged in comments.
