# Seedance 2.5

Seedance 2.5 is the current version of ByteDance Seed's audio-video generation product, the successor to [Seedance 2.0](../history/tools/seedance-2.md). The line's distinction in this wiki is synchronized multimodal output: treating audio and video as a coupled creative artifact rather than generating silent video.

## Current status (as of 2026-10-21)

- Positioned as a high-end multimodal video-generation system, a fit for creative production rather than general assistant use
- Ships inside ElevenLabs' **Chimera** template (ElevenCreative, 2026-10-21): Eleven v4 performs the voice and Seedance 2.5 animates a character's face in sync with it, from one image of a human, cartoon or pet plus a scripted line; ElevenLabs calls the pair "two of the world's leading models"
- That partner launch is the wiki's only source for 2.5 so far: no ByteDance release note, release date, pricing, length or resolution limits have been ingested

## Weaknesses / caveats

- Capabilities specific to 2.5 (versus 2.0) are undocumented in the wiki; the description above carries the product line's positioning forward from the 2.0 product page

## Recent changes

- [2026-10-21] Seedance 2.5 appears as the video model in ElevenLabs' Chimera talking-character template; supersedes [Seedance 2.0](../history/tools/seedance-2.md), which moved to history.
