# ChatGPT Pages

A ChatGPT surface for shared human/agent work, launched at OpenAI DevDay on 2026-09-29 as a companion to [ChatGPT Space](chatgpt-space.md) and [ChatGPT dots](chatgpt-dots.md). AINews lists "ChatGPT Space and Pages" together as shared human/agent workspaces.

## Current status (as of 2026-09-30)

- Shipped at DevDay as one of the companion launches to dots
- Latent Space's host summarized OpenAI's DevDay demo as one flow: edit Spaces and Pages, collaborate, and add in your dot

## Weaknesses / caveats

- The sources so far name Pages only alongside Space. They do not say how a Page differs from a Space document, what it is for, or which plans get it. Treat this page as a placeholder until a source describes Pages on its own.

## Recent changes

- [2026-09-29] Launched at OpenAI DevDay alongside dots and Space.
