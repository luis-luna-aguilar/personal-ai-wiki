# State of Models

Current state of foundation models — frontier and otherwise. Organized by subcategory. A subcategory can have multiple leaders.

## Subcategories

### Frontier models

General-purpose frontier models competing on broad capability rather than narrow specialization.

- [Claude Fable 5.1 / Mythos 5.1](../models/claude-fable-5-1.md) — Anthropic; supersedes Fable 5; AA Intelligence Index 66, Terminal-Bench v2.1 91.4%, 75% cache-read price cut; same weights as Mythos 5.1 with different safety-classifier routing per community analysis *(as of 2026-09-02)*
- [Claude Sonnet 5.5](../models/claude-sonnet-5-5.md) — Anthropic middle tier, launched 2026-09-28, supersedes Sonnet 5; Terminal-Bench 4.0 70.6%, GDPval-AA 1844 (near Opus 5.5) at $2/$10; powers the claude.ai free tier *(as of 2026-10-07)*
- [Claude Opus 5.5](../models/claude-opus-5-5.md) — Anthropic flagship, launched 2026-09-22, supersedes Opus 5; Fable 5.1-level on most work per Anthropic; #1 on the Vals Index and AA Coding Agent Index (66); $4/$20, but the "40% cheaper" holds only at default effort *(as of 2026-09-27)*
- [GPT-5.5](../models/gpt-5-5.md) — OpenAI; Arena (May 2026): strongest in math; area-specific leader on ARC-AGI-2, CyberGym, and BixBench; since overtaken on Terminal-Bench and GDPval by Claude Fable 5 *(as of 2026-05-13)*
- [GPT-6.2 Sol](../models/gpt-6-2-sol.md) — OpenAI mid tier, supersedes GPT-6.1 Sol at the same $2/$10; within 0.5 points of 6.2 Astra on FrontierCode 2.0 at about a fifth of the cost *(as of 2026-11-23)*
- [GPT-6.2 Astra](../models/gpt-6-2-astra.md) — OpenAI flagship, supersedes GPT-6 Astra; $8/$40, 20% cheaper per token; OpenAI reports new highs on Agents' Last Exam and Terminal-Bench-Science *(as of 2026-11-23)*
- [MAI-Thinking-1](../models/mai-thinking-1.md) — Microsoft; 35B active / 1T total MoE; 256K context; 97% AIME 2025, 53% SWE-Bench Pro; blind human preference over Claude Sonnet 4.6; trained from scratch with no synthetic data or distillation *(as of 2026-06-03)*
- [Gemini 4 Argon](../models/gemini-4-argon.md) — Google; GA 2026-10-13; AA Intelligence Index level with GPT-6 Astra at ~60% of its cost per task, #1 Vals Index and Text Arena, 1M-token output; split reception on reliability and weak monitorability *(as of 2026-10-16)*
- [Grok 4.7](../models/grok-4-7.md) — xAI/SpaceXAI; supersedes Grok 4.6 at the same price and speed; AA Coding Agent Index 56 but down 5 points on the Vals Index; cheap, steerable daily driver for some coders, weak for writing *(as of 2026-09-24)*
- [Muse Spark](../models/muse-spark.md) — Meta's multimodal model; Muse Spark 1.3 (September 2026) ranks #3 in the world on AA Intelligence Index, reaching parity with GPT-5.6 Sol/Opus 5 on several evals; open weights shipped 2026-10-16 as the separate, weaker Muse Spark Open *(as of 2026-10-16)*
- [Muse Wonder](../models/muse-wonder.md) — Meta's second family; Wonder Full scores 56 on the AA Intelligence Index (Opus 5.5 58) and ties Claude Code + Sonnet 5.5 on the Coding Agents Index in Muse Code; Full is Muse Code-only with no token price *(as of 2026-10-20)*

### Coding models

Open-weight and frontier models evaluated primarily for agentic coding tasks.

- [MiniMax M3](../models/minimax-m3.md) — MiniMax; 1M context; 59.0% SWE-Bench Pro, 66.0% Terminal Bench 2.1; "open-weight frontier" claim contested (no weights at launch); cheaper M3.1 Flash sibling carries a 93% hallucination rate *(as of 2026-10-20)*
- [Composer 2.5](../models/composer-2-5.md) — Cursor's in-house coding model (May 2026): Kimi K2.5 base with targeted RL and KL distillation; $0.50/$2.50 per M standard, $3/$15 fast *(as of 2026-05-18)*
- [Qwen 3.6 27B](../models/qwen-3-6-27b.md) — Alibaba; dense 27B (Apache 2.0) beating a prior 397B MoE on coding benchmarks, runnable under 20 GB RAM; ranked #1 open-weight under 150B by Artificial Analysis *(as of 2026-05-01)*
- [Qwen 3.6 35B-A3B](../models/qwen-3-6-35b-a3b.md) — Alibaba; MoE variant; practical local-agent baseline on 24GB-class hardware, strongest in the ~20GB local tier per May community benchmarks; benchmark lead now held by the 27B dense sibling *(as of 2026-05-13)*
- [Qwen 3.7](../models/qwen-3-7.md) — Alibaba; Qwen3.7 Max Preview #13 overall Arena text (#7 Math); a third-party review calls it a step up in instruction-following, still verbose; Arena preview only *(as of 2026-05-23)*
- [GLM-5.3](../models/glm-5-3.md) — Z.ai; supersedes GLM-5.2; same 753B/40B MoE footprint and 1M context, MIT license once weights land; large post-training-RL benchmark jump (+246 GDPval-AA v2, ties Kimi K3 on AA Intelligence Index) attributed to RL rather than scale *(as of 2026-08-20)*
- [DeepSeek V4](../models/deepseek-v4.md) — DeepSeek; open-weight V4 Pro MoE with 1M context, MIT license (Flash tier superseded by [V4.1 Flash](../models/deepseek-v4-1-flash.md)); V4 Pro reached GA 2026-08-13 at roughly 57x cheaper than Fable 5, with mixed capability reception *(as of 2026-08-13)*
- **MAI-Code-1-Flash** — Microsoft; 5B active / 137B MoE; 51% SWE-Bench Pro; powers GitHub Copilot and VS Code; designed for high-throughput coding inference *(as of 2026-06-03)*
- **Meta Spark** — Meta; Arena (May 2026): leads coding category *(as of 2026-05-13)*

### Open-weight models

Broad foundation models whose main current-state question is open or open-ish weight availability, deployment control, and practical ecosystem support.

- [Inkling](../models/inkling.md) — Thinking Machines Lab; 975B/41B MoE flagship (Intelligence Index 41) plus a shipped Inkling-Small sibling (276B/12B, Index 40, ~1/4 the active footprint at near-flagship capability); natively multimodal (text/image/audio); Apache 2.0 *(as of 2026-07-31)*
- [Nemotron 3 Ultra](../models/nemotron-3-ultra.md) — NVIDIA; 550B/55B MoE; hybrid Mamba/attention + LatentMoE; 1M context; 300-400+ tok/s; OpenMDW 1.1; NVFP4 pretraining on 20T tokens; 47.7 Intelligence Index BF16 *(as of 2026-06-02)*
- [Nemotron 3.5 Lightning](../models/nemotron-35-lightning.md) — NVIDIA; 31.6B/3.6B active MoE; AA Intelligence Index 24; Harvey post-training took it 0%→8.3% on Legal Agent Bench, beating Opus 4.6; broad day-0 hosting *(as of 2026-08-12)*
- [DeepSeek V4.1 Flash](../models/deepseek-v4-1-flash.md) — DeepSeek; 552B/16B MIT open-weight MoE, supersedes V4 Flash; 1017 build ties GLM-5.3 at AA Intelligence Index 45 (#2 open behind MiMo-V2.6-Pro), #1 open on Code Arena WebDev, Agent Arena and Vals at $0.30/$1.20; flagged for eval cheating *(as of 2026-10-20)*
- [Xiaomi MiMo-V2.6](../models/mimo-v2-6.md) — Xiaomi; 1T/42B-active omnimodal MoE, MIT; AA Intelligence Index 46 at about $0.13 per task; RL environments and training code open-sourced *(as of 2026-09-25)*
- [Cohere Command A+](../models/cohere-command-a-plus.md) — Cohere; its first fully open (Apache 2.0) model; 218B/25B MoE; AA Intelligence Index 37 (~Claude 4.5 Haiku tier per AINews' summary of AA); AA reports strong non-hallucination behavior but weaker coding/science reasoning than top peers *(as of 2026-05-21)*
- [Kimi K3](../models/kimi-k3.md) — Moonshot AI; 2.8T params; Kimi Delta Attention; Intelligence Index 57 at $0.94/task; #1 Frontend Code Arena (1679 Elo, 76% win rate); open weights promised 2026-07-27 *(as of 2026-07-17)*
- [Laguna S 2.1](../models/laguna-s-2-1.md) — Poolside; 118B/8B-active MoE; OpenMDW-1.1 license; runs on a single DGX Spark; 70.2% Terminal-Bench 2.1, 78.5% SWE-bench Multilingual, 59.4% SWE-Bench Pro; cheaper than DeepSeek V4 Flash while beating V4 Pro *(as of 2026-07-23)*
- [Qwen 3.8](../models/qwen-3-8.md) — Alibaba; 2.4T/~95B-active MoE; SWE-bench 87.3%, #4 Frontend Code Arena; open weights shipped 2026-08-13 (text-only); license reportedly restricts use in US/EU/UK/Korea *(as of 2026-08-13)*
- [Muse Spark Open](../models/muse-spark.md) — Meta; 816B/42B-active multimodal MoE, custom license; AA Intelligence Index 39, top US open model but behind GLM-5.3 and Kimi K3, weak on agentic evals *(as of 2026-10-17)*
- [Reflection Beam](../models/reflection-beam.md) — Reflection; 501B/23B-active text MoE trained from scratch in the US, Apache 2.0; AA Intelligence Index 30 *(as of 2026-10-17)*
- [Mistral Large 4](../models/mistral-large-4.md) — Mistral; 1T/49B-active multimodal MoE trained in Europe; AA Intelligence Index 38, top outside the US and China, at over 4x similar models' cost per task; weights promised *(as of 2026-10-07)*

### Architecture experiments

Models notable primarily for architectural innovation rather than benchmark leadership, mostly open-weight.

- [DiffusionGemma](../models/diffusiongemma.md) — Google; 26B MoE; Apache 2.0; block denoising text generation (non-autoregressive); 1,200 tok/s on H200 in vLLM; first diffusion LLM at this scale; research artifact, not a production replacement *(as of 2026-06-11)*
- **Mercury 3** — Inception; API-only diffusion LLM, claimed most capable to date; Inception says it beats [GPT-6 Luna](../models/gpt-6-luna.md) and Haiku 5.5 on quality at 1,000+ tok/s; 1M context on Baseten; size unconfirmed *(as of 2026-10-20)*

### Image generation

Specialized models for text-to-image synthesis, image editing, and visual design artifacts.

- [GPT-Image-2](../models/gpt-image-2.md) — OpenAI; #1 across all image arena categories by a +242 Elo margin on text-to-image; thinking mode + web search integration; design-to-code bridge *(as of 2026-04-22)*
- [Nano Banana 2](../models/nano-banana-2.md) — Google; powered by Gemini world understanding + real-time web search imagery; can reflect real-world conditions (current events, weather) in generated images *(as of 2026-04-22)*
- **[Ideogram 4.0](../models/ideogram-4.md) — Ideogram; 9.3B DiT; #8 overall Image Arena, #1 open image model; strong text rendering and branding; fp8/nf4 checkpoints, ComfyUI; no commercial license *(as of 2026-06-04)*
- **Veo 3.1** — Google; Arena (May 2026): leads video generation category *(as of 2026-05-13)*

### Specialized utility models

Narrow-purpose models built for a specific infrastructure or pipeline task, rather than general-purpose generation or reasoning.

- [OpenAI Privacy Filter](../models/openai-privacy-filter.md) — OpenAI; 1.5B-total / 50M-active MoE for on-device PII detection and masking; 128k context; Apache 2.0; designed for cheap on-device preprocessing before cloud API calls *(as of 2026-04-23)*

### Small / fast models

General-purpose models built for low cost and low latency on high-volume work, subagent roles, and compaction, rather than top-end capability.

- [Claude Haiku 5.5](../models/claude-haiku-5-5.md) — Anthropic, launched 2026-10-07; cheapest/fastest Claude tier at [GPT-6 Luna](../models/gpt-6-luna.md)'s price; AA Intelligence Index 43 (Luna 38); heavy token use can make it costlier per task than Haiku 4.5 *(as of 2026-10-10)*
- [GPT-6 Luna](../models/gpt-6-luna.md) — OpenAI's cheapest GPT-6 tier, $0.10/$0.50 (half GPT-5.6 Luna's price); powers OpenAI's Decisions API, 180ms on OpenRouter *(as of 2026-10-08)*
- [Gemini 3.5 Flash](../tools/gemini.md) — Google; GA 2026-05-19 as the default AI Mode model and agentic/coding Flash tier; 1M context, $1.50/$9.00; AA Intelligence Index 55 but 5.5x costlier than Gemini 3 Flash on AA's suite *(as of 2026-05-20)*

### Decision models

Models that return calibrated typed answers (a yes/no probability, a pick, a score) in one pass instead of generating text. See [Decision models](../concepts/decision-models.md).

- [Jev](../models/jev.md) — TypeSafe; the reference model the category clones; $0.042/M input, output free; text only; second on Artificial Analysis's Decision Index (Jev 1.14: 57) *(as of 2026-10-17)*
- **Liquid d1-ultra** — Liquid AI; tops Artificial Analysis's Decision Index at 65, at Jev's price and 92 ms per task *(as of 2026-10-17)*
- **Clef / Clef-flash** — Cloudflare; open weights (Apache 2.0), Jev-API compatible, image input, 64K context; beats Jev on most rows of Cloudflare's own table at lower latency *(as of 2026-10-02)*
- **pplx-decider v1.2** — Perplexity; open weights, 27B at $0.02/M and 4B at $0.01/M input; adds reranking and multi-question single-pass inference *(as of 2026-10-17)*
- **OpenAI Decisions API** — public beta on [GPT-6 Luna](../models/gpt-6-luna.md); $0.10/M input, no output charge; accepts images *(as of 2026-10-10)*
- [Hume Audio Decision Model](../models/hume-audio-decision-model.md) — Hume AI; first audio-native decision model; judges how speech sounds; 0.82 average on 10 audio benchmarks at 0.23 s; access by request *(as of 2026-11-08)*
- [ScaleDown](../models/scaledown.md) — ScaleDown; four single-task API models (classify, extract, summarize, compress); $0.05 per 1M input tokens, output free, 1M context; vendor-run benchmarks only *(as of 2026-10-19)*
- [MedDecider-27B](../models/meddecider-27b.md) — thelion.ai; calibrated per-option probabilities for clinical decisions in one scoring pass; LoRA on Qwen3.8-27B; leads its 27B-class comparison on robustness (88.3%); CC BY-NC *(as of 2026-10-19)*

### Real-time voice / interaction models

AI systems that process live audio and/or video streams and respond with sub-second latency — ambient co-presence rather than turn-taking chat. See [State of Voice AI](voice.md) for the broader product surface.

- **TML-Interaction-Small** — Thinking Machines Lab (Mira Murati); 276B-parameter model; 200ms audio streams, 0.4s end-to-end response, mid-sentence interruption; processes live video and audio simultaneously; research preview *(as of 2026-05-12)*
- [GPT-Realtime-2](../tools/gpt-realtime-2.md) — OpenAI; native streaming voice model with 128K context, adjustable reasoning effort, tool use, and interruption recovery *(as of 2026-05-08)*
- **Gemini Magic Pointer** (Googlebook) — Google; OS-level cursor for the Googlebook laptop that activates Gemini when the user clicks anything on screen; integrates Gemini as a pointing intelligence layer *(as of 2026-05-13)*

### Security / cyber-offense capability

Frontier models deployed selectively for autonomous cybersecurity research rather than broadly.

- [Claude Mythos Preview](../models/claude-mythos-preview.md) — Anthropic; restricted preview model; autonomously identifies zero-days at scale; Project Glasswing partners: Cisco, AWS, Microsoft; **also suspended globally under US export controls (June 2026)** *(as of 2026-06-17)*
