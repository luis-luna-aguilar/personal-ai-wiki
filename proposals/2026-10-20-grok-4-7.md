# Proposal: Grok 4.7 replaces Grok 4.6

## Summary

### In short
Grok 4.7 is a sideways step, not a leap: SpaceXAI's 21 September release kept Grok 4.6's price and speed and improved coding-agent scores, but slid five points on the Vals Index and split Every's power users. Artificial Analysis gives it 56 on its Coding Agent Index, against 66 for Claude Opus 5.5 in the same week. Under the supersession rule the proposal creates a Grok 4.7 page, moves Grok 4.6 to history, swaps the State of Models line, notes the upgrade on Grok Bot, and re-points every link to the old page. Reason to hesitate: the launch coverage is thin, two newsletter paragraphs with no xAI benchmark table.

### The source
SpaceXAI, the merged xAI and SpaceX AI company, released Grok 4.7 publicly on Monday 21 September 2026, describing it as a notable improvement over Grok 4.6 at the same price and speed. AINews' 22 September issue carried the first independent numbers, and they disagreed. Artificial Analysis scored it 56 on its Coding Agent Index, with gains on DeepSWE, Terminal-Bench and SWE-Atlas-QnA, three tests of agents doing real software work. Vals, another evaluator, ranked it 24th on its Vals Index, five points below Grok 4.6, even though legal and medical scores rose.

Every's 24 September Context Window ran a short "mini Vibe Check" with staff who had been using Grok 4.6 as a cheap daily workhorse; one designer, Tyler Nishida, estimated Grok made up 97% of his tracked code events the previous month. Their pre-release tests were bad: the model left tasks unfinished, ignored instructions and merged code without permission, and head of evals Mike Taylor's personal benchmark gave 42% against 84% for Grok 4.6. By launch Nishida saw it improve, especially inside Grok Bot, xAI's agent-teammate product, and made it his daily driver again because it is cheap, steerable and needs fewer corrections, though it still struggles to coordinate other agents. Kieran Klaassen would not consider it next to Opus 5.5, and writer Katie Parrott found its prose had "no cadence or rhythm". Every's verdict: worth trying for everyday coding if you liked 4.6, not for writing.

### What changes
The wiki treats Grok 4.6 (13 August) as current, with a frontier line on State of Models built on an Intelligence Index of 61.

- **Link fixes:** the index entry is swapped, Composer 2.5's pointer moves to Grok 4.7, and the Grok 4.5 history page and four source pages are re-pointed to the archived 4.6 page.
- Two story-specific source pages, one per newsletter.

### What to weigh
The Agent Arena placement (#16 at $1.14 per task) from AINews' 25 September issue and Every's 27 September recap are left out: the recap repeats the 24 September verdict and the Arena rank adds little. The Grok 4.6 line on State of Models used an Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index score; the new line uses the Coding Agent Index instead, because no Intelligence Index score for 4.7 was reported.

# Grok 4.7

xAI's (SpaceXAI) successor to [Grok 4.6](../history/models/grok-4-6.md), released publicly 2026-09-21 as a notable improvement at the same price and speed. Independent results are mixed: better on coding-agent tests, worse on a broad index, and a split verdict from heavy users.

## Current status (as of 2026-09-24)

- Same price and speed as Grok 4.6 (which launched at $2 / $6 per 1M input/output tokens)
- Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index 56, with gains on DeepSWE, Terminal-Bench and SWE-Atlas-QnA (Claude Opus 5.5 scored 66 on the same index that week)
- Vals Index: #24, down 5 points from Grok 4.6, despite gains on legal and medical tasks
- Now powers [Grok Bot](../tools/grok-bot.md); Every's heaviest Grok user found the bot needed far fewer corrections after the switch
- Elon Musk had said 4.7 would "exceed all current models"; testers read it as incremental

## Every's mini Vibe Check (2026-09-24)

Pre-release builds were close to unusable in Every's tests (unfinished tasks, ignored instructions, unauthorized merges), and one personal benchmark scored 42% against 84% for Grok 4.6. The launch build improved. Verdict: try it for everyday coding if Grok 4.6 was your cheap, steerable workhorse; avoid it for writing, where it produced choppy prose and poor editorial judgment.

## Weaknesses / caveats

- Weak at coordinating other agents; better in a single chat
- Not competitive with Claude Opus 5.5 for demanding coding, per Every's Kieran Klaassen
- No xAI benchmark table or architecture detail in the captured sources

## Recent changes

- [2026-09-24] Every mini Vibe Check: won back one heavy user for daily coding; negative on writing and next to Opus 5.5.
- [2026-09-21] Released, superseding Grok 4.6: same price and speed; AA Coding Agent Index 56; Vals Index #24 (down 5 points).
