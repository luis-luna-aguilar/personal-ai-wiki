# Qwen 3.7

Alibaba's next generation of the Qwen model line, previewed on the Chatbot Arena in May 2026. Builds on Qwen 3.6 (27B and 35B-A3B) with a substantially larger scale targeting the top-15 overall Arena bracket.

## Current status (as of 2026-05-19)

- **Qwen3.7 Max Preview**: #13 overall text Arena; #7 Math, #9 Expert, #9 Software & IT, #10 Coding
- **Qwen3.7 Plus Preview**: #16 overall vision Arena
- Alibaba ranks #6 lab in text and #5 in vision by Arena total counts
- Not yet publicly released; Arena preview access only at time of writing
- Succeeds Qwen 3.6 27B (currently #1 under 150B open-weight per Artificial Analysis) and Qwen 3.6 35B-A3B

## Why it matters

Qwen 3.7 is the first Alibaba model to reach top-15 overall in Chatbot Arena text rankings, signaling that the Qwen line has crossed from strong open-weight coding performance into direct competition with closed frontier models on general-purpose tasks including math and expert knowledge. Chinese labs are no longer trailing on general-purpose benchmarks — they are placing in the top tier.

## Caveats

- Arena placement is from a preview version; final release specs and pricing not yet available
- Arena benchmark measures human preference, not task-specific performance
