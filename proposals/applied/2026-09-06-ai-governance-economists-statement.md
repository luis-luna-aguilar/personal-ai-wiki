# Proposal: 200+ economists demand AI governance action

## Summary

### The source

Over 200 economists, including 16 Nobel laureates, signed "We Must Act Now," a public statement urging governments to actively steer AI development toward broad benefit rather than leaving it to market forces alone. A companion Verasight survey found the public receptive: 60% of respondents feel anxious about AI's rise, 89% want frontier labs required to publicly disclose safety-testing results, 81% want government authority to block potentially dangerous models before release, and 69% favor requiring AI firms to hand over equity stakes into a fund that would distribute AI's gains broadly — echoing OpenAI's own earlier proposal that the US government take a 5% stake in the company.
