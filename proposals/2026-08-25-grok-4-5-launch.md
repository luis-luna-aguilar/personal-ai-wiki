# Proposal: Grok 4.5 launches — xAI's (now SpaceXAI's) first model co-trained with Cursor

## Summary

### The source

Around 8 July 2026 Cursor announced Grok 4.5, the first model to come out of its acquisition by SpaceX/xAI (now SpaceXAI). It is a 1.5-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model, three times the size of Grok 4.3, trained jointly on trillions of tokens of Cursor's own codebase and agent-interaction data plus a broad STEM mix. Cursor pitches it as "Opus-class" at a fraction of the price ($2/$6 per million tokens, roughly half of GPT-5.6 or Opus 4.8). It went live the same day across Cursor, Grok Build and the API; Composer 2.5 stays as the smaller tier.

Artificial Analysis (via AINews) places it a solid fourth: #4 on its Intelligence Index behind Fable 5, GPT-5.5 and Opus 4.8, and on par with GPT-5.5-in-Codex for agentic coding. Its real edge is efficiency — 60% fewer output tokens per task than Opus 4.8. Two caveats: the context window shrank from 1M to 500K at launch, and Cursor admits Grok 4.5 is unfairly advantaged on CursorBench because an old Cursor snapshot slipped into training.

### What changes

The wiki's picture of xAI was a stale one-liner — Grok 4.20 "leads Arena creative writing" from May — and Cursor and Grok Build both still said the joint model was "coming." This proposal moves all of that to launched:

- **New model page `models/grok-4-5.md`** with the specs, pricing, Artificial Analysis rankings (attributed as secondhand) and the two caveats above.
- **State of Models** swaps the Grok 4.20 leader line for a linked Grok 4.5 line; the old Arena claim is kept as a parenthetical so the transition is legible. The page's Recent changes list is at its cap of 10, so the oldest entry (Opus 4.8 launch, 29 May) moves to history, and the list is re-sorted newest-first — it was out of order.
- **Cursor** and **Grok Build** get their "co-training underway" bullets rewritten as "Grok 4.5 launched July 2026." Grok Build loses its "no public benchmark data" bullet, now that there is some, and its caveat softens to "limited third-party benchmarking." Both pages move to 8 July.
- Two source pages: the Cursor blog post (primary) and the AINews recap (benchmarks).

### What to weigh

The Cursor post carries no visible date, so 8 July is inferred from AINews' coverage window — one day off is possible. Every benchmark number is AINews relaying Artificial Analysis (and AA relaying Musk for the 1.5T figure); attributed, not verified against either primary. And I dropped "default model for Grok Build" to "available in Grok Build" because neither source here says "default" — a third newsletter does, so say if you want it carried and I'll add that source.

# Grok 4.5

Positioned as "Opus-class" but faster, more token-efficient, and lower cost, and as xAI's first model trained for more than software engineering: a deliberately broad STEM/knowledge-work data mix alongside coding and agent trajectories drawn from Cursor's own usage data.

## Current status (as of 2026-07-08)

- Mixture-of-experts; 1.5T parameters, 3x Grok 4.3 (Musk disclosure, per Artificial Analysis via AINews); trained jointly by xAI/SpaceXAI and Cursor on trillions of tokens of Cursor codebase + developer-agent interaction data, plus broader STEM/knowledge-work data (Cursor blog)

Grok 4.5 is the first public output of the SpaceX-Cursor tie-up: a frontier-adjacent model trained partly on Cursor's own developer-agent interaction data, deliberately broadened beyond pure coding. It competes on cost/token-efficiency rather than raw benchmark supremacy — Musk's "Opus-class, but faster, more token-efficient and lower cost" framing, with list pricing of $2/$6 against $5/$30 for GPT-5.6 and $5/$25 for Opus 4.8 (user comparisons relayed by AINews) and markedly fewer output tokens per task.
