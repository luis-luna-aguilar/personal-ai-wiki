- [x] **[science]** Devin swarm factors RSA-260 in three weeks by building a GPU number-field-sieve pipeline

    **What it is:** In a post dated 2026-09-09, Cognition researcher Eric Lu describes pointing Devin at CADO-NFS (the open-source factoring software) to build a GPU lattice siever. Nine hours later it handled RSA-250 parameters. Over three weeks, an average of 3 and a peak of 18 parallel Devin sessions rewrote nearly every stage on GPUs (polynomial selection, sieving, block Wiedemann linear algebra, square root) and factored RSA-260 (wall time ~15.5 days, finishing 2026-09-03) plus a record SNFS factorization of 2^1277−1 (C385). Cognition calls factoring 10x cheaper than before. The author is candid about the human role: 82,702 words across 3,328 messages in 192 of 233 sessions (14,450 ACUs); "Devins themselves started 101 child sessions, and 36 received no intervention from me at all." He supplied executive function, redirected unproductive work, and built a unified benchmark and measurement system, and he says agents got more confused the further the code moved from upstream CADO-NFS.

    **Why it matters:** Absent from the wiki: no page mentions RSA-260, CADO-NFS or integer factoring, and `trends/ai-in-mathematics.md` has no computational-number-theory case. It is a still-current record result with an honest account of the human's share of swarm work. Fits `trends/ai-in-mathematics.md` and `tools/devin.md`, with a cryptography line for `state-of/cybersecurity.md` and evidence for `workflows/multi-agent-coordination-evidence.md`.
