    **What it is:** Every summarizes an AI & I interview with Stripe's Emily Glassberg Sands on the "agent-native" internet. The strongest claims: fraud has moved beyond stolen cards into stolen tokens, free-trial abuse, credits, and unpaid compute bills; AI services have high marginal costs, so stolen compute can be burned or resold quickly; the internet stack must adapt as humans act through AI interfaces and agents increasingly act as economic participants; Stripe sees top AI companies reaching $30M ARR in about 18 months, roughly 3x faster than top SaaS companies from 2018; and early agentic commerce is most comfortable around small, comparable commodities.

    **Why it matters:** This is a useful update for finance, agents, and compute-infrastructure trends. It connects payment rails, fraud detection, and agent-mediated buying into one operational framing, rather than treating agentic commerce as only a UI/product question.
