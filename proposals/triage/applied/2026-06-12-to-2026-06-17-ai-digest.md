- [x] **[agents]** Loopcraft / agent-native architecture — design loops, not prompts

    **What it is:** A clear conceptual shift crystallized across multiple sources this week: the right unit of work for AI is a *loop*, not a prompt. Three converging signals: (1) Satya Nadella's 60M-view X essay arguing the real opportunity is "building a learning loop where human capital and token capital compound" — not picking the best model; (2) AINews naming this "Loopcraft" with Andrej Karpathy and Steipete quotes on removing yourself as the bottleneck; (3) Stella Garber (Every, 2026-06-16) documenting how Hoop rebuilt their customer discovery tool agent-natively using Claude API + Slack: give the model tools + let it reason, bring the tool where people work, don't hardcode sequences.
