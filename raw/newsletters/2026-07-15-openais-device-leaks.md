**1. PrismML reveals the largest-ever AI model to run on an iPhone: **The AI lab PrismML released [Bonsai 27B](https://prismml.com/news/prismml-releases-bonsai-27b), a compressed version of Alibaba’s open-source Qwen3.6 27B that it shrunk from 54GB to under 4GB — capable of running on an iPhone 17 Pro or newer. PrismML CEO Babak Hassibi [told CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/14/apple-prismml-ai-compression-iphone.html) that Apple is evaluating the technology, though it’s unclear where that will lead. Still, it’s a notable step toward running capable AI models directly on personal devices. [Check out a demo.](https://huggingface.co/spaces/webml-community/bonsai-webgpu-kernels)

**2. IBM CEO says the AI infrastructure buildout is hurting sales:** IBM’s stock just had its worst day on record, dropping 25% after the company [warned](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/14/ibm-warns-second-quarter-earnings-fell-short-of-expectations.html) that clients are shifting spending toward AI hardware like servers, storage, and memory. CEO Arvind Krishna told investors "We did not anticipate the magnitude of the capex reprioritization.” The selloff rippled across the sector, dragging down Oracle, Salesforce, and ServiceNow as well.

**3. Weco AI claims first experimental evidence of recursive self-improvement:** AI lab Weco AI published results from AIDE², a system that spent eight unattended days rewriting its own code, creating an agent that outperformed Weco’s own hand-tuned agent that it spent two years refining. The lab frames it as the first experimental evidence of an AI system improving its own capabilities autonomously. [Read the full blog post.](https://www.weco.ai/blog/first-evidence-of-recursive-self-improvement)

## **Google DeepMind’s CEO says we’re at a pivotal moment in human history — and outlined a plan to navigate it **


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/abb1426e-1109-426f-bb67-2c1d7de5e8c1/superhumanteam_A_robot_pondering_and_scratching_its_chin_whil_e7a996ea-6ec0-4e39-9fdb-3ad4b1ae7c0c_2.jpg?t=1784054903)
Caption: 


--------------------
**The viral essay. **Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis doesn’t post often — so when he does, the internet pays attention. In a new essay, [A Framework for Frontier AI and the Dawning of a New Age](https://demishassabis.substack.com/p/a-framework-for-frontier-ai-and-the-dawning-of-a-new-age), he argues that we’re standing at “the foothills of the singularity” and believes our decisions over the coming months will determine the next phase of civilization. 

**Navigating AGI.** The essay hinges on the pending arrival of [artificial general intelligence (AGI)](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/artificial-general-intelligence), or an AI system that rivals the human brain. Hassabis argues this breakthrough (if it materializes) would rival the invention of fire or electricity. He says it could usher in “unprecedented” positive change, but admits urgent action is needed.

**Hassabis’ plan.** The Nobel laureate wants to establish a self-regulating Standards Body modeled after [FINRA](https://www.finra.org/about) — the private-industry watchdog that polices Wall Street. The goal: work alongside private labs and federal agencies to track AI’s rapid progress and mitigate risk without stifling innovation. Specifically, vetting new models before their public release.

**The timeline.** Hassabis believes AGI is “probably” only a few years away and wants to get an organization in place by the end of the year. That said, AGI remains a hypothetical milestone. No lab has achieved it, and timelines like Hassabis’ are still predictions, not certainties.

## **How to turn ChatGPT into your inbox assistant**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/d03191d2-8e02-4b18-b2d4-aaa664a0d23d/Figma_Group_1000005329.jpg?t=1784041550)
Caption: 


--------------------
* Go to [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) and switch to the ‘Work’ tab at the top of your screen

* Click on Plugins > Connect Plugins > Browse all plugins > search for ‘Gmail’ > Install

* Optionally, connect Slack and Google Drive so ChatGPT can find missing project details, files, decisions, or updates

* Once connected, use the plugins with the prompt below

**_Sample Prompt_**_: Can you check my @gmail, figure out what I need to respond to, and write drafts in my voice. Use my recent sent replies or @google-drive [writing examples] for tone. Use @slack, @google-drive, or other sources where my work happens when the email is missing the latest decision, owner, file, or blocker._

* ChatGPT will review your inbox, identify important messages, and create reply drafts for you

* Review the drafts and tell ChatGPT which emails to prioritize, ignore, or handle differently next time

* Once the drafts are useful, ask ChatGPT to schedule recurring inbox check at a specific time
