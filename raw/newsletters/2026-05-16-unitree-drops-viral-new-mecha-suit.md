## **The most important news in robotics this week**

View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/897ad1a4-6e9a-4314-b8ba-995445531d12/Group_1000005238.jpg?t=1778885174)

**1. Unitree's viral new mecha suit lets you smash through walls like a superhero: **The company has just [dropped](https://www.wired.com/story/unitree-gd01-mecha-robot/) the GD01, a giant, transformable mecha suit that switches between bipedal and quadruped modes. You can climb inside the mech and pilot it to walk, crawl on all fours, and even punch through a brick wall. Positioned as a civilian transport platform, it’s priced at $650K, and represents a genuine engineering leap past the animatronic mech statues we've seen before. You can check it out [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWOyUMJWptc&t=1s). 

**2. Figure AI's humanoid livestream pulls in millions of views on social media: **A robot sorting packages in a warehouse shouldn't really be must-see viewing, but Figure AI's 24-hour livestream has proved otherwise. The startup’s humanoid robots have entered what CEO Brett Adcock calls "[uncharted territory](https://www.businessinsider.com/figure-ai-turned-a-humanoid-sorting-packages-must-see-tv-2026-5)”, sorting through 28K+ packages over 24 hours using the Helix-02 model. Originally planned as an 8-hour test, the livestream ended up pulling in over 3M views on social media. You can watch it [here](https://x.com/adcock_brett/status/2054603963996278786?s=20). 

**3. Unitree drops an App Store for humanoid robotics: **The company just launched [UniStore](https://unistore.unitree.com/), billing it as the world's first app store for humanoid robots. Owners of the G1 humanoid robot can now [download](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20260511PD210/unitree-humanoid-robot-app-store.html) skill packages like certain dance routines, martial arts moves, and walking styles. Analysts say it could be a major shift, moving the competition away from raw hardware toward developer ecosystems.

## **How robots are transforming the world around us**

View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/2fd35116-efda-46bc-b8a4-86097515e301/Group_1000005238__1_.jpg?t=1778888099)
Follow image link: (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7tT4N67xW4)
Caption: Click here to watch Japan’s new ‘Monster Wolf’ robot in action. Photo: ABC News

🐻 **Bear Scare: **Japan is in the middle of a record bear crisis, which has killed 13 people and injured more than 200. Now, citizens are turning to a snarling, fur-covered robot called the [Monster Wolf](https://www.independent.co.uk/asia/japan/japan-robot-wolf-bear-attacks-ohta-seiki-b2975670.html), which uses 50+ randomized howls and screeches to keep predators away. Orders are piling up faster than the factory can keep up, with a two-to-three-month wait list and counting. Watch it in action [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d7tT4N67xW4). 

🪖 **On The Frontlines:** South Korea's military is facing a crushing manpower crisis, and Seoul is turning to robotics to help fill in the gap. The Defence Ministry [confirmed talks](https://www.straitstimes.com/asia/east-asia/south-korea-exploring-using-hyundai-robots-as-army-numbers-fall) with Hyundai to build a "high-tech, science-driven force," with potential roles in surveillance, reconnaissance, and logistics. Potential candidates include Boston Dynamics' Spot, Hyundai's four-wheeled MobED mobility robot, and the X-ble Shoulder exoskeleton.

🧪 **Dr. Robot: **A Japanese university has [launched](https://interestingengineering.com/ai-robotics/japan-unmanned-lab-robots-ai-automation-aist) what may be the world's first fully automated medical research lab, staffed entirely by robots. The robots handle delicate work in medical research like transferring reagents, handling temperature-controlled equipment, and running cell cultures. The university aims to grow the fleet to 2,000 robots by 2040, with similar humanoid-powered labs springing up at biotech firms worldwide.

View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/355095d8-80b3-4ea7-8cc8-28bf248417d7/Group_1000005238__3_.jpg?t=1778891220)

Disney has kept the tech behind its lifelike BD-X robots tightly under wraps, so engineering student Kayden Knapik built his own.
