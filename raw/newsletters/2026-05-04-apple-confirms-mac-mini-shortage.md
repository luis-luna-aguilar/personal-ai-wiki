Apple confirms AI agent demand is driving a Mac mini shortage:** On Apple’s latest earnings call, outgoing CEO Tim Cook [warned](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/as-tim-cook-steps-down-apple-hit-record-sales-but-a-chip-shortage-looms/) that the Mac mini and Mac Studio will likely be in short supply for months as developers snap them up to run local AI agents. **2. Meta opens its ad ecosystem to third-party AI tools:** Meta Ads AI Connectors are [rolling out](https://digiday.com/marketing/meta-opens-its-ad-ecosystem-to-third-party-ai-tools/) in open beta, letting advertisers plug ChatGPT, Claude, and other AI assistants directly into their Meta ad accounts. Meta says its business AI tools now [facilitate](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/meta-says-its-business-ai-now-facilitates-10-million-conversations-a-week/) 10 million conversations per week, up from 1 million at the start of the year.

**3. Two SaaS leaders post breakout quarters:** Atlassian [announced](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/company-news/shareholder-letter-q3fy26) revenue of $1.8B, up 32% YoY, while Twilio [posted](https://investors.twilio.com/news-releases/news-release-details/twilio-announces-first-quarter-2026-results) revenue of $1.4B — its fastest growth in three years at 20% YoY. Both companies credited AI for the surge, contradicting the narrative that vibecoded software will replace legacy SaaS companies. Read the full breakdown [here](https://www.saastr.com/atlassian-and-twilio-crush-the-quarter-accelerate-is-the-saaspocalypse-over/).

##### **FROM THE FRONTIER**

## **If an AI chatbot were transported to 1930 — could it replicate humanity’s discoveries?**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/43e54af5-167b-494a-bc10-0de73cc6cb4a/superhumanteam_A_robot_traveling_through_time_in_a_time_machi_244fc2db-0533-45e7-896e-3f7a119bfc7a_3.jpg?t=1777836849)
Caption: Made with Midjourney


--------------------
**Living in the past. **A new AI model has never heard of the computer, internet, or cell phone — and that’s not a design flaw. Researchers trained [talkie](https://talkie-lm.com/introducing-talkie) exclusively on pre-1931 content with two goals: to study past societies, and to see if AI can rediscover humanity’s greatest breakthroughs.

**Meet the vintage models.** Talkie joins a small but growing group of “vintage models” trained exclusively on historical text. [Mr. Chatterbox](https://huggingface.co/spaces/tventurella/mr_chatterbox) is a Victorian-era model built on 28,000 British Library books from 1837 to 1899. [Machina Mirabilis](https://michaelhla.com/blog/machina-mirabilis.html) goes back even further, trained on 2,600 pre-1900 physics texts.

**A glimpse into history. **[Vintage LLMs](https://owainevans.github.io/talk-transcript.html) have no knowledge of future events, offering a window into how past societies thought. They essentially let researchers step back in time to interact with someone from another era, and could unlock a deeper question about AI itself.

**Testing for AGI.** Researchers also plan to test whether vintage models can reason their way to genuinely new ideas — a focal point of AI research. If a model trained only on 1930s knowledge can independently invent the computer, that's a strong signal that artificial general intelligence (AGI) may have arrived.

## **How to generate editable infographics with AI**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/9a76124c-aae9-42e8-b0f6-389fafc2c0ed/Figma_Group_1000005220.jpg?t=1777887096)

--------------------
* Use [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) or Claude and prompt:

_“Research [topic], find outliers + strong narratives, and generate 4 infographic concepts”_

* Pick the best concepts and ask:

_“Turn each into clean image prompts with copy-ready data”_

* Paste prompts into Gemini and generate a few variations

* Download the best PNG and upload to Canva

* In [Canva](https://www.canva.com/), use Magic Studio → Magic Layers to make it editable

* Clean up text, fix visuals, improve hierarchy, and tighten layout

* Export and post across platforms
