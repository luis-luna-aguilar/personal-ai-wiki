## **A small research team broke Apple’s hardware defenses in less than a week using Claude Mythos**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/213e3861-e6b8-4959-83f8-2c01e7d28172/superhumanteam_Two_researchers_hacking_an_iPhone_--ar_169_--s_e15bd3a5-9a2a-4b97-943c-7ac02fa36ded_1.jpg?t=1779060652)
Caption: Made with Midjourney


--------------------
**Five days.** Apple spent five years and likely hundreds of millions of dollars developing its latest hardware security system. A small team — armed with Anthropic’s still-unreleased Claude Mythos model — just [broke](https://blog.calif.io/p/first-public-kernel-memory-corruption) it in less than a week.

**The break-in. **Last Thursday, researchers at Calif published [visual evidence](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tH-4u9Jbl_g) of the first public memory corruption exploit bypassing Apple’s Memory Integrity Enforcement (MIE) on M5 chips — a defense Apple touted as its strongest-ever protection against memory attacks. The team then hand-delivered a copy of their report to Apple’s HQ in Cupertino.

**Working together. **Calif relied on Mythos to surface the bugs quickly, but says human expertise was essential for bypassing MIE itself. The takeaway, per the team, isn’t that Mythos achieved this on its own. It’s that small groups paired with frontier AI can now pull off what used to require entire organizations.

**AI bugmageddon.** Calif's framing is blunt: “Apple built MIE in a world before Mythos Preview." The two teams are now working to patch the vulnerability. But if even Apple's strongest-ever defense can fall in days, it's a preview of what might be coming for every other system.

## How to analyze data in Excel using ChatGPT


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/056ee8a6-09f2-48a7-91fd-60ab99fb069d/Figma_Group_1000005241.jpg?t=1779028802)
Caption: 


--------------------
* Open [Excel](https://www.microsoft.com/en/microsoft-365/excel) and click Add-ins in the Insert tab

* Search ‘ChatGPT’ > ‘Add’ to open it in the sidebar

* Click ‘Continue with ChatGPT’ > Login with your ChatGPT account

* Upload your data (Excel, CSV, image, PDF or anything) and start prompting

**_Sample Prompt:_**_ “give me a formula to calculate the total sales in column C for values above 500.”_

* You can also prompt it to build financial models

* Ask questions, get formulas, or generate analysis in plain language, and it turns them into real, usable results for you
