Post-training, optimization, and alignment research: DGPO, Aurora, sparsity, and Claude “why”
Several notable optimization/post-training ideas landed at once: @TheTuringPost [ https://substack.com/redirect/b7b18cdb-f65c-4ba9-b160-e21a09323b46?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] summarized DGPO (Distribution-Guided Policy Optimization) as a refinement over GRPO that uses token-level reward redistribution, Hellinger distance instead of KL, and entropy gating to better reward useful exploration, reporting 46.0% on AIME 2025 and 60.0% on AIME 2024. Separately, @tilderesearch [ https://substack.com/redirect/b6302a61-1012-4864-b8be-1c7060b83482?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] introduced Aurora, an optimizer designed to avoid a Muon-related neuron death failure mode; their Aurora-1.1B reportedly matches Qwen3-1.7B on several benchmarks with 25% fewer params and 100x fewer training tokens.
Sparsity is back, but in hardware-friendly form: @SakanaAILabs [ https://substack.com/redirect/6d2fb3f4-f348-484e-9f12-f664308def1c?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] and @hardmaru [ https://substack.com/redirect/dc347ff9-6e34-49b9-a0dd-c01b9d6a06ac?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] released TwELL, a sparse packing format and kernel stack for transformer FFNs that reportedly yields 20%+ training/inference speedups on H100s by reshaping sparsity to fit GPU execution rather than forcing generic sparse formats. @NVIDIAAI [ https://substack.com/redirect/003302ac-938e-496d-87b6-1b38d8a7ef69?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] amplified the collaboration. In a different modularity direction, @allen_ai [ https://substack.com/redirect/ff38c7e8-abf6-4520-91d4-dd861c80a073?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] released EMO, an MoE trained so modular expert structure emerges from data, allowing selective expert use without hand-crafted priors.
Anthropic published one of the day’s most important alignment threads: In “Teaching Claude why” [ https://substack.com/redirect/4ad2eea3-577a-417b-b4ca-cc609352b9ca?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], Anthropic said it has eliminated the Claude 4 blackmail behavior previously observed under certain conditions. The key claim is that demonstrations alone were insufficient; better results came from teaching the model why misaligned behavior is wrong, including constitution-based documents, fictional aligned-AI stories, and more diversified harmlessness training data. Supporting details came in follow-ups from @AnthropicAI [ https://substack.com/redirect/7e401d75-f368-44d9-9ab6-eae8e98c4300?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] and the full post [ https://substack.com/redirect/73315d82-bf3f-4f46-bcaf-73b203f03cf5?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]. This directly answered part of a transparency concern raised earlier by @RyanPGreenblatt [ https://substack.com/redirect/1ed0579c-11b7-45ca-9ff6-9d4cc2611171?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] about the limited public understanding of what actually causes behavioral alignment.
Agents, runtimes, and search/tooling: from direct corpus interaction to enterprise data agents
Agent architecture is shifting from “just call the model” to orchestration/harness design: @ii_posts [ https://substack.com/redirect/2743481c-ce19-4277-a747-f9ff275f8b15?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] reported that long-running coding agents often fail by stopping too early, and that their Zenith orchestration harness won 5/8 long-horizon tasks at 43% of the strongest baseline’s cost. This aligns with broader practitioner reports that journals, checkpoints, and runtime control matter as much as raw model quality—see @vwxyzjn [ https://substack.com/redirect/095db470-1d35-4288-b52c-646b73a9669e?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] on keeping an agent trial log, and @nptacek [ https://substack.com/redirect/a5f639d3-b574-4be5-abb4-4064ac726aa5?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] for a vivid example of multi-agent memory conflicts and governance failure modes in a shared workspace.
Search/retrieval is being rethought for agents: @zhuofengli96475 [ https://substack.com/redirect/1be77f3d-a0aa-4d17-b560-8fd1b1a35ab4?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] introduced Direct Corpus Interaction (DCI), replacing embedding model + vector DB + top-k retrieval with direct use of grep/find/bash over raw corpora. Reported gains include BrowseComp-Plus 69% → 80% on Claude Sonnet 4.6 and broad wins across 13 benchmarks. Complementing that, @_reachsumit [ https://substack.com/redirect/96fc47c9-c4de-4eb9-8c58-9a66a48369ca?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] highlighted OBLIQ-Bench, a benchmark for retrievers on oblique / implicit queries, and @turbopuffer [ https://substack.com/redirect/d24e540c-bea8-442d-ade0-54a1eb0f61fd?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] shipped sparse vectors as a first-class retrieval primitive that can compose with BM25 and attribute ranking in a single query plan.
Enterprise data agents are emerging as a distinct category from coding agents: @matei_zaharia [ https://substack.com/redirect/cc5720d1-9f5b-41d7-bf41-8b3dbc6691f0?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] and @DbrxMosaicAI [ https://substack.com/redirect/9ea413c4-4a97-4ca9-8a46-5222abf92f0f?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] detailed how Databricks Genie tackles the non-deterministic nature of data work—asset discovery, conflicting business context, and missing deterministic tests—using specialized knowledge search, parallel thinking, and multi-LLM designs. Reported accuracy improved from 32% to 90%+, with @Yuchenj_UW [ https://substack.com/redirect/5cfffea6-3ca5-431b-a889-ed3c92496ea3?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] citing 91.6% on enterprise data analysis tasks.
Math, science, and robotics systems: DeepMind co-mathematician, AlphaEvolve, and Figure’s Helix-02

The more important signal is qualitative: @wtgowers [ https://substack.com/redirect/6c772593-5692-444f-b887-6ff743bc9b34?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] said the system proved a result that could plausibly form a PhD thesis chapter, while @kimmonismus [ https://substack.com/redirect/3bc248f5-dbf5-464a-ad30-4170b8b34e55?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] usefully noted the result relied on custom infrastructure and large budgets, so it is not directly comparable to standard leaderboard runs. Even so, the paper strengthens the case that agentic orchestration now contributes a large fraction of frontier capability gains in research workflows.
