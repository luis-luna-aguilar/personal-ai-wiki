AI Twitter Recap
Meta Releases Muse Spark Open, the Top-Scoring US Open-Weight Model
Release: Meta released Muse Spark Open, an 816B-total / 42B-active MoE with a 1M-token context window. It accepts text, image, video and audio input and is aimed at coding, long-horizon agents and multi-step reasoning (announcement [ https://substack.com/redirect/6f515d96-bbcf-4941-bb82-dfadde468e1a?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], Wang thread [ https://substack.com/redirect/3b0eff81-0c31-41de-a3cd-6a2d3e9d45cb?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Lineage: Jack Rae says it was the first base model trained under the new MSL org, with additional training for the open release (Rae [ https://substack.com/redirect/af42bc8e-d5b6-44f0-9014-65e1e6659726?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]). Several posters describe it as roughly Muse Spark 1.2 level (Yuchen Jin [ https://substack.com/redirect/fd773ace-1f77-42d9-ac9a-afbf2ac4022e?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], TimDarcet [ https://substack.com/redirect/5d923812-0572-4afd-a428-fa02efabd7b1?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
License: The weights ship under the custom Muse Model License. It requires “Built with Muse Spark Open” attribution and a separate agreement for Model-as-a-Service businesses with more than $20M in annual revenue (AA [ https://substack.com/redirect/43e7adfb-a822-4605-b052-308bcc0ad6a9?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]). Nathan Lambert called the license “trash” (natolambert [ https://substack.com/redirect/1fe781d2-2d91-42c0-8ee7-a0aa332a6792?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Architecture: According to vLLM, the model has 62 decoder layers, all MoE, with 256 experts per layer and 8 active per token. A latent-MoE pooler projects the hidden state from 8,192 to 4,096 for expert computation and back again. Attention is hybrid: 46 layers use a 2,048-token sliding window and 16 attend to the full context. Image and video go through a Perception Encoder, and audio uses a separate 118M encoder (vLLM arch [ https://substack.com/redirect/20d1c5be-5357-4dd2-885a-9a1c405fad1d?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Design comparison: Elie Bakouch notes that Muse is the only major US open model without shared experts. It also uses gated residuals, about 9 norms per layer and muP-like multipliers, including a router factor of 1/sqrt(number of experts) (overview [ https://substack.com/redirect/ec4b8f26-2355-40b6-b491-e0747cd012c4?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Independent measurement: Artificial Analysis scores it 39 on its Intelligence Index. That ranks it first among US open-weight models but behind GLM-5.3 (45) and Kimi K3 (44) (AA [ https://substack.com/redirect/43e7adfb-a822-4605-b052-308bcc0ad6a9?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], Rundown [ https://substack.com/redirect/33820b67-5490-458f-b8ff-396eeb3b6d44?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Strengths: It uses 40k output tokens per index task, the fewest among the nine highest-scoring open models. It also leads open models on AA-Omniscience (25).
Weaknesses: It ranks last among those nine on the index’s four agentic evaluations. GLM-5.3’s entire 6-point lead comes from that agentic segment.
Pricing and deployment: Launch pricing on Together, Fireworks and Baseten is $1.25/$4.25 per 1M input/output tokens ($0.15 cached input). AA calculates this at $0.84 per index task, versus $2.01 for GLM-5.3 and $0.27 for DeepSeek V4.1 Flash. Together offers a free endpoint until Oct 18 (Together [ https://substack.com/redirect/0c68d5a6-d69e-4197-9a22-9a1129b1087d?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Hardware footprint: BF16 weights are about 1.6TB. The NVFP4 checkpoint quantizes only the experts and comes to about 530GB, which runs on 4× Blackwell Ultra, 8× H200, or two 512GB M5 Ultra Mac Studios via MLX (AA [ https://substack.com/redirect/43e7adfb-a822-4605-b052-308bcc0ad6a9?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], Meta [ https://substack.com/redirect/670e9918-7eca-4198-a527-b816809578e5?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Ecosystem: llama.cpp had GGUF support on day 0 (Delangue [ https://substack.com/redirect/c677977a-8137-45b1-9676-0f1b98cb16b5?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]), and Unsloth published quants, with UD-IQ1_S at 176GB (Unsloth [ https://substack.com/redirect/d8b5930c-ac7f-4bdf-a70b-84fb0d2f77ed?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
vLLM day-0 stack: vLLM support covers NVIDIA, AMD MI355X (FP8 and MXFP4) and TPU (vLLM [ https://substack.com/redirect/2b2bf096-b334-4523-8d99-49dbf116de90?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
TPU throughput: Megakernels with DSpark speculation (acceptance length 6) exceed 650 tok/s on 4 TPU v7 chips and 960 tok/s on 8 (hardware [ https://substack.com/redirect/25a83787-9af4-4adc-8de2-2477ddf6f43e?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Speculator: Inferact trained a block-diffusion DSpark drafter that proposes up to 7 tokens per pass (speculator [ https://substack.com/redirect/d7146f8d-5ee5-4fb1-a9b4-f6462086d46a?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).

Over 150 steps on 16 GB300s, AIME-2024 mean@8 rose from 0.56 to 0.85, with train-rollout KL around 2×10⁻⁴ (RL [ https://substack.com/redirect/237000ed-cc5d-47f5-9d35-dd3332acbcc9?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Reception: Lambert says the release cuts the US–China open-model gap by about half (natolambert [ https://substack.com/redirect/b6915090-f58e-4634-b131-586f4404bbf8?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]). Teortaxes calls it “grim at this param count” with “obsolete attention” and notes that Spark 1.3 is substantially stronger; these are attributed opinions (teortaxes [ https://substack.com/redirect/fc155cd7-fd08-4e07-9e2c-ceea1d994681?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], Spark 1.3 comparison [ https://substack.com/redirect/640b8876-fccc-45a9-a7a3-a453c7f9d3b8?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Other Model Launches: Nemotron 3.5, MAI-Base-2, Gemini 4 Argon, Image Models
NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL: The model is a 120B / 12B-active hybrid Mamba-Transformer MoE VLM with a 1M-token context window and native MTP (NVIDIA [ https://substack.com/redirect/1894bb58-f2c2-42f5-93f6-5f6f9bef72f5?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Training recipe: The tech summary describes a 1T-token distillation from a larger Nemotron 3 teacher, followed by agentic SWE mid-training on 65.4B tokens at 256K context. Post-training uses multi-teacher MOPD, and a final MTP-boosting step raises acceptance length by 21.8% to 4.61 (Gordić [ https://substack.com/redirect/1dce3ffd-5079-41c8-844f-4bc1a0fcab2f?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Claimed results: NVIDIA reports 51.78% on SWE-bench Pro and 4,856 output tok/s per GPU on GB200.
Independent evals: AA scores Super VL 26, the leading US open model before Muse, with NVFP4 within 0.1 points of BF16. The companion Nemotron 3.5 Bolt (74B-A9B, native 2-bit, 24GB) scores 21 (AA [ https://substack.com/redirect/e369aedb-3dcb-40f2-a2ee-e8a7e4f05d99?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).

Interpretability, Safety and Eval Integrity
Goodfire’s “already clean” essay: Nick Cammarata argues that most neurons in models up to trillions of parameters are legible. His 2023 OpenAI automated-interpretability effort found only about 2% legible, and he says many famous polysemantic examples were misread (Cammarata [ https://substack.com/redirect/202e78c3-550e-4c2d-aac7-60c44e231ca0?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], Goodfire [ https://substack.com/redirect/70cc02a9-9af0-49d2-88b9-643cf4f52ad5?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Endorsements: tszzl and others called it a major moment (tszzl [ https://substack.com/redirect/74d302d5-f42a-4794-83fc-05fab4db238f?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Pushback: Neel Nanda says it does not change his cruxes. He still expects representational superposition and believes weak activations carry memorized facts (Nanda [ https://substack.com/redirect/6c8f7362-75ac-4839-82c8-11854863ddaf?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Further critiques: Leo Gao argues explanations should be judged by neuron simulation measured with KL (nabla_theta [ https://substack.com/redirect/1548e1ce-5e5e-4c90-85cf-a52721de30a7?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]). Jake Mendel flags the neglected tail of non-max activations (Mendel [ https://substack.com/redirect/b6044bac-4c35-4d66-a286-5d5412952e0d?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
