Meta updates its research tool that turns brain waves into sentences:** [Brain2Qwerty v2](https://ai.meta.com/blog/brain2qwerty-brain-ai-human-communication/) can analyze raw brain signals captured via a noninvasive scanner and decode sentences in real-time at 61% accuracy. It’s Meta’s highest-performing brain-to-text tool yet, trained on roughly 22,000 sentences from 9 volunteers each recorded for 10 hours. The stated goal: to benefit people who can no longer speak after a stroke, accident, or brain disorder. [Learn more here.](https://facebookresearch.github.io/brain2qwerty/)

## **The frontier vs. open-weight debate is heating up**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/89bcbf82-e1bc-4300-878c-862c2df2f24e/superhumanteam_Two_robots_arguing_and_shaking_fists_--ar_21_-_6801fb2c-572d-49da-a77b-d3be5fbc641a_2.jpg?t=1782774633)
Caption: Made with Midjourney


--------------------
**The growing debate.** For years, there wasn’t much competition when it came to using a closed or open-weight model. Proprietary closed models, like Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini, were more powerful than open-weight models by some distance, so the decision was easy. But that’s changed abruptly in the past few months, with several open-weight contenders closing the gap — and giving corporate America a decision to make.

**What’s the difference?** Open-weight models are published freely for anyone to download, so you can host them privately, customize them, and keep data local on your own computer. Proprietary models like GPT-5.5 and Claude are closed: you can only access the model through the company that owns it, usually through a chatbot platform or API.

**Why go open-weight? **Open-weight models offer three benefits: more control, privacy, and often lower long-term costs than proprietary models. For years, none of that mattered because the models weren’t as strong. But today’s open-weight models have grown highly competitive, with Zhipu AI’s GLM-5.2 ranking 6th on Artificial Analysis’ [leaderboard](https://artificialanalysis.ai/leaderboards/models) — closely followed by MiniMax-M3 and DeepSeek V4 Pro.

**The decision. **Some companies, like Uber and ServiceNow, [blew through](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/02/uber-caps-employee-ai-spending-after-blowing-through-budget-in-four-months/) their entire AI budgets just months into 2026. One option some are weighing is shifting workloads to cheaper, open-weight models that match performance while cutting costs. That said, the trade-offs in performance, security, and engineering overhead mean it isn't the right fit for every team.

Sequence structure:
1. Message 1: Gentle check-in with added value
2. Message 2: Address a likely objection indirectly, without naming it
3. Message 3: Create soft urgency using a real, believable reason to act now
4. Message 4: Respectful close that leaves the door open

Requirements:
* Each message should be under 80 words
* Sound human, calm, and confident
