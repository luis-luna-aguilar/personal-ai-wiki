# [AINews] Reflection Beam - 501B-A23B American Open Model

**From:** AINews <swyx+ainews@substack.com>
**Date:** 2026-10-06

View this post on the web at https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-reflection-beam-501b-a23b

It’s been over a year since Reflection launched with us [ https://substack.com/redirect/ed69ce43-9ba2-4eb6-a359-e1a2b5281f8f?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] with big goals on coding (and hinted about their RL approach [ https://substack.com/redirect/500042a7-106d-4b7c-a4ec-9cd2134104dd?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]):
But they stayed “stealth” longer than Thinking Machines and it was not clear we would ever get a model launch out of them, as the broader open model ecosystem did not slow down one bigt for them. Well, we did [ https://substack.com/redirect/84cc60dd-38ea-45dc-87ba-46c5858bfe18?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]:
They compare themselves to Inkling, Nemotron, and GLM 5.2, but the SOTA GLM 5.3, Kimi K3, Qwen 3.8 Max, and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash are generally ahead. Still, since this is US-trained from scratch, there’s a segment of the market that has been eagerly waiting for more options here, and more importantly, Reflection has now announced its arrival as a functional neolab!

AI Twitter Recap
Reflection’s Beam Leads a Wave of Open-Weight Releases
Beam launch: Reflection announced Beam, a text-only 501B-total / 23B-active MoE for coding, agentic and scientific work. It was trained from scratch, and full weights under Apache 2.0 are due this month (announcement [ https://substack.com/redirect/ac0f8c83-22fa-4f9c-b5f4-ef4b2ff835dd?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], Laskin [ https://substack.com/redirect/4c9ae616-3e96-4c2a-95ca-b0096d8c12a2?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Training scale: Team posts cite 23.8T pretraining tokens, partly from an OCR pipeline over hundreds of millions of PDFs (data lead [ https://substack.com/redirect/80823e6c-5f5a-480f-805b-99b7412d2cb3?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]). They also describe a stable RL/OPD run on 10K GB300s with more than 100M rollouts across ~1M tasks (Damos [ https://substack.com/redirect/136b6499-547f-43b5-b29b-648f361822e0?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Claimed results: A summary of Reflection’s claims gives 80.9 on SWE-bench Verified, 3–4x the inference efficiency of GLM 5.2, and four weeks each of pretraining and RL on ~10,500 GB300s (summary [ https://substack.com/redirect/8b1e4725-62c7-4c45-a000-c8a463d136e3?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]). A tech report and OSS integrations are promised (Polozov [ https://substack.com/redirect/1c38eea1-a488-47d4-9749-710a5e2d0e16?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Context: Axios reported the launch ahead of time. It said Reflection pays $150M/month for Colossus compute plus a $1B Nebius deal, and that other unnamed US labs will ship open models this month (Curran [ https://substack.com/redirect/421b7b7c-ee1a-4c7b-9538-445891a3176f?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Independent and critical reads: Artificial Analysis has early access and expects Beam to be among the most token-efficient open models for its intelligence (AA [ https://substack.com/redirect/a1e0b133-7f0c-4220-b1f1-34f186d5b8d9?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
MFU and architecture: Elie Bakouch estimates only ~12% BF16 MFU in pretraining. He reads the architecture as 3:1 interleaved global/sliding-window attention and notes better held-out code perplexity than DSv4 (analysis [ https://substack.com/redirect/61ab25a1-54b4-4d71-b7e2-7be49a0e10a3?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Compute comparison: Teortaxes calls Beam an iso-FLOP replication of DeepSeek V3 (post [ https://substack.com/redirect/53b92bed-be1e-4cef-92fb-dfcf6af84257?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]). He infers ~1.3B RL sandboxes over 4 weeks, with up to 170K running at once (sandboxes [ https://substack.com/redirect/9f931051-e65b-4c38-9dc1-51d6c86e9360?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Positioning: Observers place Beam around GLM-5.2 level (iScienceLuvr [ https://substack.com/redirect/e0a32c19-e32f-4984-80c5-f966936fbd97?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]) and below DSv4 Flash on some benchmarks (critique [ https://substack.com/redirect/6bfac86a-cfc0-449b-935a-d53933cfb2bb?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]). Nathan Lambert groups it with Nvidia and Thinking Machines as strong US releases that still trail Chinese counterparts (Lambert [ https://substack.com/redirect/4a56a160-2158-41e0-92bf-99666b309c1b?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).

Agent Harnesses, RL Environments and Developer Tooling
Multi-harness RL (Hugging Face): A capture proxy speaks the OpenAI Chat, OpenAI Responses, Anthropic and Gemini formats. It forwards calls to vLLM and records exact token IDs and logprobs for TRL, so 10 unmodified harnesses become RL environments (Delangue [ https://substack.com/redirect/a5cfecaf-9b68-469c-bb9d-d77262d842be?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], explainer [ https://substack.com/redirect/30f960b6-abb4-45cf-b9c0-3337396e94dd?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Results: The same weights score 62% under Mini-SWE-Agent and 33% under Claude Code. Training LFM2.5-2.6B across 4 harnesses lifts first-attempt solves from 42% to 54%, and a tool-call bonus cuts calls by 31%. SFT on 3,189 rollouts plateaus at 47.5%.
Caveats: The run used one task family and one seed.
Environment hosting: RL environments are now hosted and versioned on the HF Hub like datasets (blog [ https://substack.com/redirect/60019828-b9e4-4cab-8fd1-804bc777364a?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Pi Durable: Earendil’s harness is built around a small task-based workflow engine, so long-running, multiplayer agents can suspend and resume anywhere (Pi [ https://substack.com/redirect/e3f83a0b-2b77-4f0d-8ee8-7bb11b2782c3?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Design: The core is ~15K lines of TypeScript with SQLite/JSONL storage and runs on Bun or Cloudflare Durable Objects. Control is separated from execution environments (review [ https://substack.com/redirect/e401b618-7316-4b2e-9065-8ff4f99fe8da?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Effect.ts: The authors explain they skipped Effect because it does not provide durability (Zechner [ https://substack.com/redirect/0dac7e85-01d2-4550-9bf3-ac9ea10b1973?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).

Agent and Training Research
Verification over sampling:
NVIDIA mid-harness: The method samples candidate shell commands and verifies them before running one. A GPT-5.6 Sol verifier choosing among 8 actions lifts TerminalBench-Lite Pass@1 from 50% to 68%, while weak verifiers add little (summary [ https://substack.com/redirect/be19f282-d4f1-4066-a766-f4d7060fb0e6?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Google VeriHarness: The method challenges claims that all rollouts agree on and resolves disagreements against workspace evidence. It adds +6.2 points with Gemini 3.5 Flash and +6.4 with Opus 4.8, and ~26K rollouts are released (summary [ https://substack.com/redirect/c74aeea1-99e2-4927-96e3-e2d33b89dbe6?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Context management:
UT Austin compression study: Across ~35K runs, compression that uses a third of the tokens can be 20–80% slower than full context. Threshold triggers beat step triggers, and the best policy varies by model (summary [ https://substack.com/redirect/08056216-5982-4e97-9d98-0c2026514b64?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
PAIR: The method replays an agent from the same state to isolate harmful compressions. It then rewrites the compression prompt and comes close to no-compression performance (paper [ https://substack.com/redirect/6cf2e25c-9156-47c7-a20d-036ac07e2e5e?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
CorpusMap: Precomputed entity pages for document collections raise answer quality 6.4–11.7 points while cutting input tokens 34–57% (paper [ https://substack.com/redirect/1b0fcfcd-b7a5-4e20-a593-b8499bd70f35?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
