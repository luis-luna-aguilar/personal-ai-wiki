AI Twitter Recap
Google used I/O to reposition Gemini as both a consumer AI surface and a developer/agent platform, with three core technical announcements: Gemini 3.5 Flash for fast agentic/coding workloads, Gemini Omni for multimodal generation/editing starting with video, and a broader Antigravity agent stack spanning desktop/CLI/SDK/API. Official posts emphasized scale — Google says it now processes over 3.2 quadrillion tokens/month, up 7x YoY from 480T/month, while the Gemini app has 900M+ monthly users and is available in 230+ countries and 70+ languages (Google [ https://substack.com/redirect/e71a825d-eb31-46e9-81dc-358e72fb432a?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], Google [ https://substack.com/redirect/6c293925-1380-4201-870a-648ae9f3f2fe?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], GeminiApp [ https://substack.com/redirect/ad44cad5-dcd4-4bd1-8c7a-223713c81e1f?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]). Google paired that with Gemini Omni, a new family combining Gemini reasoning with generative media, initially via Omni Flash, capable of taking text/image/video/audio inputs and producing video edits/generation in Gemini, Flow, Shorts, and later APIs (GoogleDeepMind [ https://substack.com/redirect/7f7423d7-99be-472d-b326-d2cf49945571?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], Google [ https://substack.com/redirect/7c5f52e0-a5c2-48e1-a24f-929842905555?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], GeminiApp [ https://substack.com/redirect/3f1033ed-ff67-4477-ab67-cbc12125bd87?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]). Implications
The notable shift is that Google appears to be using a “Flash” label for a model that, in prior cycles, would have been described more like a high-end product model optimized for deployment rather than simply a cheap lightweight tier. Several posters called this out directly, arguing Flash is becoming more expensive and possibly absorbing former Pro territory (enricoros [ https://substack.com/redirect/36a5a09d-710c-4c3f-aa1d-a6fdaba90dbe?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], simonw [ https://substack.com/redirect/53c51f9c-8b0e-42d5-939c-b965fe193b30?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
The strongest technical signal is not “best absolute benchmark model,” but:
material agentic gains
extreme serving speed
deep integration into product surfaces
tooling built around subagents and long-horizon execution
That makes 3.5 Flash strategically important even if some competitors still win on raw price-adjusted intelligence in certain third-party comparisons.
Gemini Omni: multimodal generation/editing as “create anything from any input”
What Google announced
Google introduced Gemini Omni as a new family merging Gemini reasoning/world knowledge with Google’s generative media stack, starting with video creation and editing. Official messaging described it as “create anything from any input,” but current rollout is narrower:
Inputs: text, images, audio, video
Initial output emphasis: video
Product availability: Gemini app, Flow, YouTube Shorts/Create, later APIs
Current shipping model: Gemini Omni Flash (GoogleDeepMind [ https://substack.com/redirect/7f7423d7-99be-472d-b326-d2cf49945571?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], Google [ https://substack.com/redirect/1d5e8922-4d83-4d5a-8006-2afb74d2e92a?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], Google [ https://substack.com/redirect/91d24e03-69f9-450d-b5c7-0a070e3cee4c?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ])
Google/DeepMind claims:
Better world understanding
More robust physics
Multi-turn editing where scene/character consistency is retained
Ability to “reimagine” user video footage with conversational edits (Google [ https://substack.com/redirect/d85be61a-00dc-4a9b-8c31-52d226374d94?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], Google [ https://substack.com/redirect/1c827475-08ec-4063-b97c-06583d54c836?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ])
Rollout specifics:
Paid Gemini users globally in app/Flow “today”
YouTube Shorts/Create rolling out “starting this week” at no cost
APIs for developers/enterprise in coming weeks (Google [ https://substack.com/redirect/91d24e03-69f9-450d-b5c7-0a070e3cee4c?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], GeminiApp [ https://substack.com/redirect/5d6c2a41-6244-44d0-a9ae-ca382b2e34dc?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ])

Context
Omni matters less as “yet another video model” and more as Google’s attempt to unify:
multimodal understanding,
media editing,
world grounding,
agent interfaces,
and eventually any-input/any-output generation.
This aligns with DeepMind’s long-running world-model agenda and Google’s product distribution advantage.
Antigravity: Google’s agent OS, not just a coding assistant
A major underappreciated I/O theme was that Google is no longer presenting agents as a thin wrapper around a chat model. Antigravity is becoming the execution substrate.

Technical signaling
Google’s own demos centered on parallel sub-agents, hosted execution, high-frequency iterative loops, and artifact-oriented workflows. Jeff Dean explicitly described 3.5 Flash as a strong engine for “deploy sub-agents that collaborate, run high-frequency iterative loops, and solve real-world problems at scale” (JeffDean [ https://substack.com/redirect/0e609ee4-7057-4133-b76e-4326be0e4d00?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
The marquee proof point:
OS built in 12h
93 parallel sub-agents
15k+ requests
2.6B tokens
< $1K credits (Google [ https://substack.com/redirect/3e60ab71-d25f-4f62-8e1c-b3d0a6c1442a?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ])
Even if this is mostly a stage-managed benchmark/demo, it reveals the architecture Google wants developers to adopt: many fast agents over one slow monolithic run.
Reactions
Positive: this is Google’s answer to Codex/Claude Code/OpenClaw/Hermes-style workflows, with a stronger infra story (iScienceLuvr [ https://substack.com/redirect/9c096a0b-d330-451d-89ba-86cbb0f2deb0?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], theo [ https://substack.com/redirect/075da2a8-2601-4713-965e-ccf9681f36c8?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).

Trust, provenance, and standards
Google pushed SynthID across Search, Gemini, Chrome, and hardware/media surfaces, and announced partnerships with OpenAI, NVIDIA, Kakao, and ElevenLabs to bring SynthID to their generated content (Google [ https://substack.com/redirect/41514185-f68e-4f94-a38d-2554dc88c770?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], Google [ https://substack.com/redirect/6de7b1d5-5d53-459b-817c-01fc3ab0003a?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
That is one of the more consequential standards moves from I/O:
it gives Google a shot at owning part of the provenance layer for generative media;
notably, OpenAI separately announced support for checking OpenAI-generated images via SynthID watermark + C2PA credentials (OpenAI [ https://substack.com/redirect/b6fc6590-ea7d-4970-8542-5f1a458d1745?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
This was less flashy than Omni/3.5 Flash, but likely more durable if provenance becomes mandatory infrastructure.

Why this matters
Google now has a coherent deployment story.
Earlier Gemini cycles often felt benchmark-heavy and product-fragmented. At I/O, Google tied model, infra, tools, APIs, consumer surfaces, and enterprise rollout together.
The center of gravity is shifting from chatbot UX to agent execution.
The important primitives were not just model IQ: they were subagents, hosted sandboxes, long-running tasks, generated artifacts, and integration with Search/Workspace/Android.
Gemini 3.5 Flash suggests “fast enough to orchestrate many agents” may matter more than max benchmark score.
For coding and tool use, throughput and latency are increasingly product-defining.
Omni reveals Google’s differentiation thesis.
Google is betting on multimodal/world-grounded systems rather than purely text-centric competition.
Trust/provenance is becoming platform infrastructure.
SynthID partnerships with OpenAI/NVIDIA/ElevenLabs/Kakao suggest some convergence around content-auth provenance layers.
The biggest unresolved question is economics.
Technically strong or not, 3.5 Flash drew substantial pushback on cost inflation. If “Flash” is no longer the cheap workhorse tier, Google may win on capability deployment while losing some developer mindshare on predictability and pricing simplicity.

Training Algorithms, Benchmarks, and Agent Evaluation
RL/post-training discussion is shifting toward denser credit assignment: @nrehiew_ [ https://substack.com/redirect/867313ed-3c01-4e82-9c90-17f6abe0d1a9?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] argued that the next scalable training breakthrough may build on GRPO but with denser, lower-bias credit assignment, citing directions like ECHO, Composer2, self-distillation, and OPD. @lateinteraction [ https://substack.com/redirect/0e80a8e7-ec49-472c-9d73-6ccd6cedfd5a?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] countered with a “pedagogical RL” framing: train a self-teacher that samples correct and easy-to-follow rollouts.
Can coding agents do research? Not yet: Intology AI [ https://substack.com/redirect/c66324fb-eebe-4ea1-bcc8-96b0a8219373?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] released NanoGPT-Bench, an autonomous benchmark based on the NanoGPT Speedrun competition, testing whether coding agents can contribute to real AI R&D progress. Science, Biology Models, and Domain-Specific Systems
Hugging Face releases Carbon DNA models: One of the most technically interesting open releases was Carbon [ https://substack.com/redirect/fd5a8510-5aea-4b48-988a-999401f101a6?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], a family of generative DNA foundation models. The team says Carbon-3B matches Evo2-7B while running 250–275x faster at inference, enough to process the whole human genome on a single GPU in under two days. The key recipe changes: deterministic 6-mer tokenization, a factorized loss (FNS) replacing plain cross-entropy late in training, and curated staged mixtures of functional DNA + mRNA data per @LoubnaBenAllal1 [ https://substack.com/redirect/6282cafb-109f-4257-85fb-3e2fcb0515cf?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ].
