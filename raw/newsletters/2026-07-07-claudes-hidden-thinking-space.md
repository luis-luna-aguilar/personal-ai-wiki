**1. Anthropic reveals Claude has a mental workspace:** New [research](https://www.anthropic.com/research/global-workspace) from Anthropic found that Claude often works through concepts without writing them directly in its chain-of-thought, much like how a person might think about one topic while doing something else. The concept, called a J-space, formed unintentionally during training and now helps power Claude’s higher-level reasoning. [Watch how it works](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rKV5JcALQoQ) or test out a [live demo of a J-space.](https://www.neuronpedia.org/qwen3.6-27b/jlens)

**2. Chinese labs keep piling on the pressure:** Tencent released [Hy3](https://hy.tencent.com/research/hy3), a strong-for-its size open-source model, while Meituan fully open-sourced [LongCat-2.0](https://longcat.ai/blog/longcat-2.0/) — the first trillion-parameter coding model allegedly trained on 50,000 Chinese chips rather than Nvidia GPUs. Both land as cheaper, competitive alternatives to US models, while signaling China's steady push to cut its reliance on American silicon.

**3. Nvidia's next flagship AI rack delayed until 2028:** The chipmaker's Kyber system — a cabinet that fuses 144 of its most powerful Rubin Ultra chips into a single computer for training frontier models — has [reportedly](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/06/nvidia-kyber-rack-system-delays-manufacturing-taiwan-rubin-chips-.html) been pushed from 2027 to 2028, according to SemiAnalysis. If true, it’s the latest in a string of product setbacks for Nvidia, although the tech giant has denied the report.
