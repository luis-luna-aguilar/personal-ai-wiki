Architecture and systems details
Official specs: 2.8T total parameters, 1M context, native multimodal input (text + images), text output, open weights by July 27 @Kimi_Moonshot [ https://substack.com/redirect/d2c354e9-961b-4d40-bd58-1117af3e278f?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], @ArtificialAnlys [ https://substack.com/redirect/134590cc-fb00-4161-bf38-202c7abe41c6?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]
K3 uses Kimi Delta Attention (KDA), which Moonshot says enables up to 6.3x faster decoding in million-token contexts @Kimi_Moonshot [ https://substack.com/redirect/d2c354e9-961b-4d40-bd58-1117af3e278f?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]
