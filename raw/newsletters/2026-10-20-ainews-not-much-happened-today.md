DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 Becomes the Open-Weight Value Leader
Release and specs: Artificial Analysis lists DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 as a 552B-total / 16B-active MoE model with a 1M-token context window and an MIT license (AA [ https://substack.com/redirect/79eaa06c-d8c3-4014-8271-285f6bb62747?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Pricing: Unchanged at $0.30/$1.20 per million input/output tokens during peak hours. Off-peak pricing is half that, and cache hits cost $0.006 per million tokens.
Availability: It is available through DeepSeek’s API, Fireworks, Baseten, DeepInfra, Cloudflare and Together.
Training claim: Together says DeepSeek trained the update with self-feedback RL for building complete projects from scratch (Together [ https://substack.com/redirect/6cf2686c-fa76-44a1-b893-82175960f859?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Artificial Analysis results: The model scores 45 on the AA Intelligence Index, up 6 points. That ties it with GLM-5.3 for #2 among open-weight models, 1 point behind MiMo-V2.6-Pro, at $0.26 per task.
Agentic and science gains: GDPval-AA Elo rose 146 to 1746, and CritPt rose from 14% to 24%.
Hallucination: On AA-Omniscience, the non-hallucination rate jumped from 4% to 33% (detail [ https://substack.com/redirect/10762c25-d1c6-4f11-979c-809867dde203?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Caveat: AA notes that MiMo-V2.6-Pro and GPT-6.1 Sol (medium) both score higher at a lower cost per task.
Arena and Vals rankings: The model is the #1 open model in Code Arena WebDev, placing #12 overall with 1670 points. That is 52 points above the prior Flash and roughly 10 points behind GPT-6 Sol Max (Arena [ https://substack.com/redirect/5fcc0220-01d7-45b0-aac1-2a9dd2461c99?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Agent Arena: It is also #1 open in Agent Arena, at #11 overall with a $0.20 median cost per task. Arena says that delivers about 37% of GPT-6.1 Sol’s improvement at 27% of the cost (Agent Arena [ https://substack.com/redirect/5a60b637-fd01-44e6-8620-1111ac26d5e0?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Vals Index: It initially ranked #1 open-weight at $0.64 per task (Vals [ https://substack.com/redirect/a7f49411-cc8d-4344-97d6-02f18bd543b1?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]). Under Vals Index v2.2 it ranks #8 overall and remains the top open-weight model (v2.2 [ https://substack.com/redirect/2bafc9df-9e8b-4dac-8e58-c648ab31daa5?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Browser use: It scored 75/100 on long-horizon browser use, matching Opus 5.5, at $0.22 vs $2.35 per task (browser_use [ https://substack.com/redirect/afb82a48-3cb2-44d1-ad49-6870543f5f77?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Hands-on and contested evals: A Zhihu tester found the larger gains in agentic execution than in reasoning (ZhihuFrontier [ https://substack.com/redirect/37d4b85c-03e0-4233-a53f-289920bb2919?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).

Agent Harnesses and Research Methods
Harness² (Google Research): The open-source framework lets an agent edit its own harness (system prompt, skills, hooks, memory) while the model weights stay frozen (akshay_pachaar [ https://substack.com/redirect/d1d3dcef-c2e0-41c3-99e9-e3438f1e9c7a?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], _philschmid [ https://substack.com/redirect/2a5022a6-4feb-4fa9-b4d2-6bd7550c7918?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
Method: An editor agent proposes candidate edits, a composer diffs trajectories against a baseline, and a reflector saves lessons for future tasks. No grader is needed.
Results: Gemini 3.5 Flash gains 8.4 points on WorkBuddy-Bench and 7.5 on JobBench. Skills and scripts make up about 50% of edits.
Agent memory: A paper compresses document collections into 16 memory tokens for a frozen LLM. It beats full context on FactKG (84.33% vs 80.43%) while using 108× fewer source tokens (omarsar0 [ https://substack.com/redirect/a92ec214-ba20-4b3e-ba81-917cba4d0e80?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]).
