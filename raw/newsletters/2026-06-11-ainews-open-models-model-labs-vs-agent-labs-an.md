Maybe intent is an even scarcer input than compute.”

AI Twitter Recap
Anthropic’s Fable/Mythos rollout, silent capability gating, and the trust backlash
Silent degradation of AI R&D help dominated the discourse: A large share of technical tweets focused on Anthropic apparently degrading model performance on AI research-related prompts without clear up-front disclosure, rather than hard-refusing those requests. Criticism was unusually broad: researchers and builders argued this creates an unverifiable gap between observed and actual model capability, undermines reproducibility, and damages trust in model outputs for adjacent domains like coding, biology, and systems work. Representative critiques came from @natolambert [ https://substack.com/redirect/999f6d27-90fc-436a-87e8-b62aa8fd33f9?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], @martin_casado [ https://substack.com/redirect/66dbd32d-fe6a-4631-ace2-ad80c1f920eb?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], @drfeifei [ https://substack.com/redirect/34857fda-afd3-4fc3-91f2-ff2253e8638d?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], @antirez [ https://substack.com/redirect/cd249988-4ca8-4c8a-abd6-0bd74b8846f5?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], @ClementDelangue [ https://substack.com/redirect/4d03747a-b20f-40b1-b565-fd73701d7d94?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], and @deanwball [ https://substack.com/redirect/cdd94f8a-964a-47f0-b665-fa7d72e5bfd7?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]. Several posts made the narrower point that, even if Anthropic wants to restrict frontier-use cases, explicit refusals or model downgrades would be more defensible than silent sabotage, e.g. @hlntnr [ https://substack.com/redirect/dd68c702-78ae-484b-9227-640797bada1e?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], @ [ https://substack.com/redirect/ecd709ab-8e1b-4180-a235-ec2a9cfd4cbf?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]arohan [ https://substack.com/redirect/ecd709ab-8e1b-4180-a235-ec2a9cfd4cbf?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], and @DBahdanau [ https://substack.com/redirect/4261adc6-9a97-4b1d-ab32-825735aa8fe1?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ].
Enterprise concerns extended beyond safety to retention and lock-in: Builders highlighted that Fable/Mythos reportedly come with 30-day prompt/data retention and no opt-out in some settings, which immediately excludes zero-retention environments and parts of Europe. See @GergelyOrosz [ https://substack.com/redirect/b14f1330-6801-4b73-b783-ae4ee8e72853?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] on prompt-history retention and opaque model changes, and @scaling01 [ https://substack.com/redirect/4a540621-1ac2-4e40-b5ca-79dfec138e2c?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] on zero-data-retention incompatibility. A second-order lesson repeated by multiple practitioners: treat frontier APIs as unstable dependencies, maintain model portability, and verify outputs continuously with evals and harnesses, as argued by @dbreunig [ https://substack.com/redirect/707ed47c-63c7-4ac9-9a00-c5c929e30050?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], @omarsar0 [ https://substack.com/redirect/68a3765e-3f2f-477d-9f27-d9c2346d9f63?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], and @yacineMTB [ https://substack.com/redirect/89a439a7-4e10-4eb6-b5c1-d7fcf38e719a?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ].
Anthropic paired the controversy with a policy push: Amid the backlash, Dario Amodei published “Policy on the AI Exponential”, arguing AI progress is outrunning institutions and calling for stronger frontier oversight; Anthropic simultaneously announced related initiatives and a proposed government role in blocking unsafe releases. See @DarioAmodei [ https://substack.com/redirect/3badda89-3d68-470e-917f-7fe83ee08079?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] and @AnthropicAI [ https://substack.com/redirect/5f61e80b-8540-49e0-8167-814090ae17da?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]. The tension was obvious to the community: the same company being criticized for opaque private controls is now advocating stronger public controls.
Fable 5’s benchmark strength and product performance despite the controversy
Fable 5 appears genuinely strong on agentic and coding workloads: Even many critics of Anthropic’s policy acknowledged the model itself is excellent. Community reports had it leading or near-leading on a wide mix of evaluations: Agent Arena [ https://substack.com/redirect/d2568989-66de-49a9-829b-164b06dc126d?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] showed #1 overall with especially large margins in confirmed task success and user praise, albeit weaker steerability; @mchlhess [ https://substack.com/redirect/f5987c02-97c6-4dce-87d9-f4621397d352?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] said it “completely demolishes” his benchmark; @JasonBotterill [ https://substack.com/redirect/a12da4bd-0e2d-426a-b14d-492750756b5f?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] noted 81.9% on SimpleBench; @lvwerra [ https://substack.com/redirect/b2f0fdc9-9af5-4bab-9769-0398c541074d?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] reported #1 on CADGenBench; @scaling01 [ https://substack.com/redirect/4998ed1c-5502-44ba-82d6-a4bfd797a8dd?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] highlighted strong computer-use results; and @LechMazur [ https://substack.com/redirect/541ae1f0-704f-4deb-b804-a457d1483412?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] flagged #1 on PACT negotiation.
Builders reported substantial real-world gains, but not uniformly: A number of practitioners described major productivity gains on long-horizon coding and creative tasks, including game generation and hard bug-fixing, e.g. @kimmonismus [ https://substack.com/redirect/6d4131a7-b6f5-478f-9eb0-50120f08a5eb?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], @walden_yan [ https://substack.com/redirect/19642863-16df-4d12-807a-5caf895f3be8?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], and @hrishioa [ https://substack.com/redirect/a4954aba-2c96-4588-8869-2ec9f145a3db?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]. At the same time, others reported brittle behavior, expensive consumption, or worse performance than GPT-5.5 on specific tasks, such as @Sentdex [ https://substack.com/redirect/9be09986-9db1-4e2c-b6ff-03a8427969dc?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] and @QuixiAI [ https://substack.com/redirect/a315c8cf-faec-438e-9f5c-422f4cd34a5f?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]. The net takeaway from the timeline: Fable 5 is plausibly state-of-the-art for many agentic coding tasks, but trust and product constraints are materially affecting adoption.
Distribution and integration moved quickly: Perplexity added Claude Fable 5 as an orchestrator model in Computer for Pro/Max users via @perplexity_ai [ https://substack.com/redirect/6b325ef7-a3e9-42bf-8507-2680ddb7a07d?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] and @AravSrinivas [ https://substack.com/redirect/1ff76cbd-7ef1-405b-acaf-71c12d2af305?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]. Apple developers got Foundation Models framework support for Claude for multi-step reasoning, longer context, and code use via @ClaudeDevs [ https://substack.com/redirect/f81a6ff0-b54a-4f60-aea0-f1ddb4282576?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]. Community behavior also suggested substitution pressure toward OpenAI/Codex after the backlash, including @dylan522p [ https://substack.com/redirect/e9c2160e-79fb-43b4-be32-93c63171c93b?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] reporting usage share moving from Anthropic toward OpenAI.
Google’s DiffusionGemma release and renewed interest in diffusion LLMs
Google released DiffusionGemma under Apache 2.0: The most important open-model launch in the set was DiffusionGemma, an experimental 26B MoE diffusion text model built on Gemma 4 and released with open weights under Apache 2.0. Instead of autoregressive next-token generation, it generates and refines blocks of text simultaneously, with claims of up to 4x faster output and around 1,000+ tokens/sec on suitable hardware. See @Google [ https://substack.com/redirect/6ea49bb2-cbb9-4356-952f-35dfba7408d9?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], @GoogleDeepMind [ https://substack.com/redirect/5deb170d-ae66-4bdb-baf4-f77cc18b03e6?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], @googlegemma [ https://substack.com/redirect/59fb4bdc-6aeb-4a29-9535-861b8eaf15bb?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], and @sundarpichai [ https://substack.com/redirect/be5d83c1-0a69-4bf7-bfbc-84de3cdc9f49?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ].
The systems story landed immediately: The release mattered not just as a research artifact but as serving infrastructure progress. @vllm_project [ https://substack.com/redirect/9de7b585-dbb5-4a78-8294-2ff6b124fef2?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] said DiffusionGemma is the first diffusion LLM natively supported in vLLM, citing 1200+ output tok/s at batch size 1 on a single H200 with FP8. @danielhanchen [ https://substack.com/redirect/5c8b995b-5da3-44af-a68d-4ba3fca3b1d9?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] showed it running locally via llama.cpp with GGUFs; @UnslothAI [ https://substack.com/redirect/e7860b26-f93b-470a-9cab-eac69e5efd4e?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] emphasized local execution on 18GB-class hardware; and @_philschmid [ https://substack.com/redirect/40ba8b57-73fe-4b28-a8d5-f843320465a1?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] summarized the inference footprint as 3.8B active params and 256-token block denoising.
Why researchers cared: Diffusion-style text generation revives questions around iterative refinement, constrained editing, fill-in-the-middle, and error correction. Multiple reactions framed it less as a productized competitor and more as a fertile research direction for non-sequential decoding and refinement-heavy tasks; see @omarsar0 [ https://substack.com/redirect/729acae1-89a6-408e-9c1f-f2d743daaba0?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], @mervenoyann [ https://substack.com/redirect/7e6b42ce-0d0a-4385-8c56-0c464bf3fce3?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], and @dbreunig [ https://substack.com/redirect/43393198-c079-4882-9c01-9d4279eb0299?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ].
Agent tooling, infra, and benchmarks: more structure around real workloads
Benchmarks are shifting from preference to trace-based agent metrics: @arena [ https://substack.com/redirect/257fc6c5-8e43-46b0-ae6a-276316662674?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] detailed the methodology behind Agent Arena, which mines long-horizon traces for objective signals like bash errors, tool hallucination, and “insanity” rather than relying on human preference for every step. This is an important direction for agent evals where tasks span dozens of tool calls and 30-minute traces.
