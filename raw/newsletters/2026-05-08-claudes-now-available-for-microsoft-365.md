## **The compute war is heating up as Anthropic strikes back**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/47e4775c-09a9-45ba-bb86-81018bf6e6ff/superhumanteam_Two_robots_about_to_box_--ar_169_--sref_httpss_311fd179-063f-4ab7-baf8-f87f02752330_2.jpg?t=1778194926)
Caption: Made with Midjourney


--------------------
**The compute war. **Behind every LLM query is a cluster of GPUs running billions of parameters in real time to spit out an answer. For AI labs, more compute means better models, higher usage limits, and more customers served. In 2026, it’s the constraint the entire industry is fighting over.

**Anthropic’s bottleneck. **Anthropic’s [business](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/06/anthropic-ceo-dario-amodei-says-company-crew-80-fold-in-first-quarter.html) surged 80X in Q1, a jump it wasn’t quite prepared to handle. And users felt it: Claude Code hit limits quickly and customers complained that the models deteriorated in quality. OpenAI’s CEO Sam Altman was prepared for the moment.

**OpenAI pounces. **Sam Altman has spent two years locking in compute, racking up $1.4 trillion in data center commitments across Nvidia, AMD, Oracle, AWS, and Stargate. Now he’s weaponizing it — resetting Codex limits almost weekly and giving away elevated usage limits to attract disgruntled Anthropic users. Sam’s [message](https://x.com/sama/status/2051671472142512190): “Come for the rate limits, stay for the models.”

**Anthropic strikes back. **This week, Anthropic secured xAI’s entire Colossus 1 supercomputer in Memphis, locking in 220,000+ Nvidia GPUs. It doubled Claude Code’s five-hour limits the same day. The compute war is in full force — and the margin for error is shrinking by the quarter.

## **How to create presentations with AI in PowerPoint**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/0122c0ac-c2cb-4e6a-8ad1-5216edf3de4c/Image_from_Squoosh__77_.jpg?t=1778153662)
Caption: 


--------------------
* Open [PowerPoint](https://powerpoint.cloud.microsoft/en-us/)

* Click Add-ins in the Home tab

* Search "Claude by Anthropic"

* Install it and open the sidebar

* Select Opus 4.6 as your model (the latest model)

* Upload your data file (Excel, CSV, anything)

**_Prompt:_**_ "Turn this attached file into a professional presentation."_

* Wait for a few minutes, and your presentation will be ready
