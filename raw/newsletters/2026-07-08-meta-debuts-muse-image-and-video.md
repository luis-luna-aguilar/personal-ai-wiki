## **Cloudflare wants to put a paywall on the agent internet, a move that could redirect billions in online revenue**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/fd88b8cb-f5fd-4b4c-9ca6-0dcdbfe4aed7/superhumanteam_A_person_building_a_wall_that_keeps_out_a_robo_0ba75d70-fda5-487c-a8fe-c7aaa18e8ef4_0.jpg?t=1783463007)
Caption: Made with Midjourney


--------------------
**Monetization Gateway. **It’s not often that a blog article from an internet security company gets 4M+ views. That’s what Cloudflare just pulled off when announcing [Monetization Gateway](https://blog.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/?utm_campaign=cf_blog&utm_content=20260701&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_source=twitter): its new platform that lets you put any digital resource (web pages, proprietary datasets, APIs, or MCP tools) behind a usage-based paywall. If widely adopted, it could reshape business models across the internet.

**The 30-year promise.** For decades, most websites have run on a simple trade-off: publish content, attract visitors, and profit from them, usually through advertising, subscriptions, or products. It’s a balanced ecosystem. Owners are incentivized to post quality content for free, knowing they can eventually monetize the audience it attracts. But that model is breaking.

**The agent internet.** More than 50% of today’s internet traffic is now non-human, with Cloudflare [reporting](https://blog.cloudflare.com/agentic-internet-bot-report/#the-agentic-internet-is-here) a 1,700% jump in daily AI agent requests from 2025 to 2026. That’s great for consumers, who get free information from AI tools in seconds. But not so great for website owners, since agents don't click ads or buy subscriptions.

**The bottom line. **Most companies have already pivoted to generative engine optimization, or writing content in hopes of getting cited by AI chatbots. But with the monetization gateway, Cloudflare has proposed another solution, one that charges an agent every time it taps a website’s resources.
