Since our founding in 2024, World Labs has built leading AI spatial intelligence capabilities for everything from creative work to design. We built the world leading model training team for images, video and spatial reconstruction. And, with the acquisition of SceniX [ https://substack.com/redirect/40dbf466-4cfc-4e72-b210-6eb254f8ba8c?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], we’re building towards an industry leading capability for robotics simulation.
Recently we released Atlas [ https://substack.com/redirect/3bf153b3-f945-4a81-bdad-b891f306f06b?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], a first of its kind omni model architecture that solves a key outstanding problem in spatial intelligence: new camera view prediction. Like LLMs can predict the next token from a line of text, Atlas, trained from scratch, can predict the next view from an input of 2D images, outperforming state of the art results even by specialized models. It has essentially solved a long standing problem in computer vision called sparse reconstruction, by combining generative models with multiview geometry. This has direct and far reaching consequences: from design and engineering to science and robotics.
We’ve seen incredible interest in Atlas across many domains: RL environments for robotics; scene generation for therapy and entertainment; and real world reconstruction for real estate, design and construction, and so much more to come.

Top Story: Claude Sonnet 5.5 launch and reactions
What happened
Anthropic shipped Claude Sonnet 5.5, the second model in the Claude 5.5 family, one week after Opus 5.5 and the day before OpenAI DevDay. Early independent evals place it at or near Opus 5.5 on several leaderboards.
Launch timing: Pre-launch chatter came first. @kimmonismus [ https://substack.com/redirect/55f16a3d-a603-4f1f-9036-ac3f00209ed6?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] reported it already routing to his account, and @scaling01 [ https://substack.com/redirect/249e0f55-b9f7-460d-8840-50c0636691b0?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] spotted it in the Anthropic API before the official post.
Official announcement: @claudeai [ https://substack.com/redirect/29f861aa-558b-439a-a957-0f0ba6480691?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] (53K engagement) and @AnthropicAI [ https://substack.com/redirect/4f256212-5bf2-457f-88df-5f223c40ce68?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] called it “a clear upgrade over Sonnet 5.” They claim it runs more than 30% faster and costs up to 30% less for most work.
Positioning: @ClaudeDevs [ https://substack.com/redirect/e94bf038-f464-4733-ab65-d7fa1e116f9d?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] positions it for “well-scoped everyday tasks like fixing bugs and quickly iterating on features.” Anthropic also published a build guide [ https://substack.com/redirect/4dd7d954-a4ea-4bb1-afc4-ea34a37ad7a5?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] covering when to pick Sonnet vs. Opus 5.5, migrating from Sonnet 5, and tuning effort.
Free tier: @simonw [ https://substack.com/redirect/f867aca8-6461-4f6b-8a8e-4428114aa239?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] points out Sonnet 5.5 now powers the free tier on claude.ai. ChatGPT’s free tier is still GPT-5.6 Luna, which he calls “a lot less capable.”
Anti-distillation change: @ClaudeDevs [ https://substack.com/redirect/b6e8c8d1-93de-4df9-bdec-13e9480dc631?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] extended “preserved thinking” to counter distillation via account-switching.
