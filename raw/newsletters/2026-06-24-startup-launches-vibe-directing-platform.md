## **Test first, build later? AI has dropped the cost of experimentation to nearly zero in many fields**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/97b3261f-7ea8-41cb-8cc7-928f2f868023/superhumanteam_A_robot_experimenting_in_a_science_lab_--ar_21_b4a3f274-3e66-4208-87fd-e9c2056fe7e8_0.jpg?t=1782234355)
Caption: Made with Midjourney


--------------------
**The Kickstarter raise.** For 15 days, Ethan Buck’s Kickstarter campaign for his miniature construction kit startup, [BYLT](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/bylt/bylt-real-construction-tiny-scale/posts/4725254), sat stagnant. That changed overnight when he posted a sleek [**AI-generated launch video**](https://x.com/ethanabuck/status/2068578138158960668) that pulled in 5M+ views and helped [raise](https://x.com/ethanabuck/status/2068896743895523591) $30K+ for a product he’s still finalizing. The story is equal parts feel-good founder’s journey — and a preview of how fast the rules are changing.

**Moving quickly.** Announcing a company using a launch video or product mock-up is nothing new. But what’s changed is the cost, time, and effort involved to make them. Bringing a prototype to life used to require weeks of work, input from various specialists, and, naturally, extensive meetings. Now the same process takes an afternoon and a few AI tokens. 

**Collapsing barriers.** AI has collapsed the time it takes to go from idea to polished asset. While Ethan’s win was a product launch, the pattern fits any role: a marketer drafting landing pages, an accountant prepping tax plans, or a product manager testing new features. 

**The bottom line.** Higher-level work will always require careful thought, attention, and human input. But as the cost of experimentation falls to near zero, the smart play may no longer be refining ideas carefully over weeks — but shipping them early and often.

## **How to turn messy notes into a structured document with ChatGPT**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/39c4478c-4663-4f46-9dc8-1f6029d461d8/image__6_.jpg?t=1782225074)
Caption: 


--------------------
* Open [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) and upload a clear photo of your handwritten notes

* Ask ChatGPT to read the image and extract the key points

**Sample Prompt:** _Keep the main points in order, group related ideas together, and use simple headings. Clean up messy wording, but don’t add anything that isn’t in the notes. If something is hard to read, mark it as [unclear]._

* Review the draft and ask ChatGPT to improve the structure

**Follow up with: **_Make this draft easier to read, remove repetition, and turn it into a polished meeting recap with action items._
