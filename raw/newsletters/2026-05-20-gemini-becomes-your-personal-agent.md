## **Elon Musk loses landmark lawsuit against Sam Altman — but plans to appeal decision**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/0ef25c67-0ff4-4043-9c3e-f71c4eee0cac/superhumanteam_A_robot_judge_ruling_over_a_courtroom_with_two_195ac7e1-028c-462a-ad66-8168bb92efcd_0.jpg?t=1779206771)
Caption: Made with Midjourney


--------------------
**The trial.** Elon Musk, Sam Altman, and Greg Brockman just wrapped up a three-week courtroom battle that featured corporate secrets, private emails, and personal diary entries. The issue on the docket: Did OpenAI betray its nonprofit mission and mislead Musk when it [converted](https://www.engadget.com/2175740/the-jury-in-the-openai-case-has-ruled-against-elon-musk/) to a for-profit entity?

**The background.** Musk was an early backer of OpenAI, investing roughly $38M during its early years. He accused Altman of pitching OpenAI as a nonprofit that was “developing AI for the benefit of humanity” — only to later convert it into a for-profit venture now valued at $852B. In Musk’s words, Altman “stole a charity.” 

**The testimony.** Musk's lawyers painted Altman as deceptive, citing ex-CTO Mira Murati's description of him as someone who'd "say one thing to one person and completely the opposite to another." There were plenty of mic-drop moments, and the WSJ collected all the “[juiciest evidence here](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/elon-musk-openai-trial-what-to-know-b104450f?st=gTRc4M&mod=superhuman).”

**The verdict. **The 9-person jury [rejected](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/jury-sides-with-openai-sam-altman-in-case-brought-by-elon-musk-933240ff?st=XNNvK3&mod=superhuman) Musk’s claims in under two hours, ruling that the statute of limitations had expired — a decision Musk plans to appeal. The ruling clears a major obstacle ahead of OpenAI's expected IPO later this year.
