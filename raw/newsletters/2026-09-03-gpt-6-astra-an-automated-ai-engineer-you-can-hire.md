# GPT-6 Astra: an automated AI Engineer you can hire for <$6 an hour

**From:** Latent.Space <swyx@substack.com>
**Date:** 2026-09-03

View this post on the web at https://www.latent.space/p/astra

GPT-6 Astra, the first Stargate [ https://substack.com/redirect/c14a0759-5e35-42fc-a4b7-209c6cb3aa45?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] and lightly looped [ https://substack.com/redirect/499532ef-de69-46e8-90e4-800fc71e8e7d?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] supermodel from OpenAI, launched today [ http://GPT-6 Astra’s launch ], cleanly beating Fable 5.1 [ https://substack.com/redirect/3ea24955-8a70-4702-8994-f6cf94dadc93?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] on many metrics including completely saturating the hardest versions of FrontierMath [ https://substack.com/redirect/5fa02967-206d-4c3c-a15e-6f44b8c44cbf?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] (97.6%) and ARC-AGI-3 [ https://substack.com/redirect/02506894-5691-46d3-8e18-dd75cb9c9d6e?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] (99.9%). Lots of demos will focus on typical talk tracks like the computer use [ https://substack.com/redirect/229eec5b-c89b-4bb5-acdb-b8f0c36927f0?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] to the Pokemon playing [ https://substack.com/redirect/ef18bcde-4130-4510-82f3-4ffa5bd982c1?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] to Blender [ https://substack.com/redirect/f15dcca0-a46d-4ef0-9814-55ec9f10e03f?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] to the scientific [ https://substack.com/redirect/65c6c345-55a8-4ad2-ad16-8b9cc897e432?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] and cybersafety [ https://substack.com/redirect/e6e8c8e3-23cb-4979-83ba-784a64a80aab?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] benchmarks (system card [ https://substack.com/redirect/48f74bb2-c0b5-43c1-a4f1-fc17f536f87d?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]). Greg says AGI is here [ https://substack.com/redirect/8fb81fca-788d-4c67-b76d-57e83d43a632?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], and Jakub says it is finally the Automated AI Research Intern [ https://substack.com/redirect/0c781eca-6eec-484d-8110-ee4f474ba4cb?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] he wanted. 
We aren’t qualified to talk about those, but we got early access and threw it at every practical, real-life task we could think of. After burning over 20B tokens of Astra, we can confirm the most surprising finding: GPT-6 Astra is one of a new class of models that are fully capable AI Engineers in their own right. They now help you choose and train models, label data (both helping you label and then using your labels for active learning, like SAM [ https://substack.com/redirect/2c5f3bca-1205-4258-ba97-875db0e4270b?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]), keep pipelines saturated, instrument and read logs, deploy and debug entire systems in one shot, fan out and command and eval subagents (including agents running other models), and keep coherence over billions of tokens of a single agent thread.
Raising Your Ambitions
We’ve written before about the high-return activity of raising your aspirations for LLMs [ https://substack.com/redirect/bcfcf022-e606-4a3d-9bfb-7d491f3dc543?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]. Our experience has made us exponentially more ambitious than we have ever been. Over the past month, we went from prompting humans for a fun “Kill My SaaS [ https://substack.com/redirect/a6543827-06e2-42bc-9a89-fb9522db40de?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]” competition, to building a dozen internal/personal tools [ https://substack.com/redirect/1929b073-d863-4192-b726-43afd434806a?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], including 4 previously paid SaaS tools [ https://substack.com/redirect/cae53667-2d85-480f-aa78-c49f2baa5bf3?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], fully redesigned my personal site [ https://substack.com/redirect/22c9a5a2-851d-4d3c-9118-28de04aead92?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], made an incomplete but functional replacement of GitHub + Vercel [ https://substack.com/redirect/c6455d8f-bb97-4faf-bec8-a60475241e25?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], trained game AI for a strategy board game with 10,000x more legal moves than Go [ https://substack.com/redirect/0a823a33-ba4c-4bb5-89a4-cf63bf154928?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], saved tens of thousands of dollars in personal finance cleanups, republished my old book [ https://substack.com/redirect/1d2833f4-dc73-4ff0-8ef8-55cb491ab498?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] with synced audiobook audio and printed physical editions, and even more [ https://substack.com/redirect/b285b0be-9424-45c4-b3b8-65c5e470f9be?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] ambitious [ https://substack.com/redirect/974e3640-8158-4c2f-8c99-9d1d47b58d0f?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] projects we will launch soon.
The $6 an hour number might sound surprising, but that’s exactly what we saw in our testing [ https://substack.com/redirect/5a6a0cbf-4604-485f-8a60-aa96062dc451?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] - 33 tokens per second at a max $50 per million token rate. Given that Astra is more token efficient than Sol and Fable (independently confirmed by Artificial Analysis [ https://substack.com/redirect/1418853f-95bf-4204-8889-68f3968dd5da?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]), it often means that Astra is simultaneously also the best fast-and-smart model you can buy (assuming our preview latency holds for GA), outside of Spark 1.3 [ https://substack.com/redirect/590689ab-1813-4ba5-b832-6046917f9eb0?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ].
Managing fleets of subagents (individually tweaked, bounded concurrency)
