##### **FROM THE FRONTIER**

## ==**The CEO behind GTA 6 argues AI can never generate true creativity**==


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/09e57587-caad-4508-ab27-ff1d921a5baf/superhumanteam_A_robot_creating_a_video_game_--ar_169_--sref__299b12a6-d155-437f-a233-a550bcb3ff96_1.jpg?t=1779339824)
Caption: Made with Midjourney


--------------------
**Can AI be creative?** Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick — the man behind Grand Theft Auto (GTA) — says no. He [argues](https://x.com/MarioNawfal/status/2056170401831796778) that AI is great at generating assets, but not hits. Sure, AI could clone GTA. But clones don’t sell.

**Backward-looking.** Zelnick's structural argument: AI is just “big datasets, lots of compute, and a large language model.” The problem? A model trained on everything that already exists cannot produce anything genuinely new (at least for now). Creativity is forward-facing: it requires spotting gaps in the market and generating a novel concept to fill that space.

**Selective adoption. **Take-Two has a mixed approach to AI. GTA 6 is 100% handcrafted, but the company is also [running](https://thenextweb.com/news/take-two-gta6-no-generative-ai-handcrafted) 200 internal AI projects across testing and productivity to find where AI might be a fit. Zelnick [sees](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/consoles-pc/gta-6-owner-take-two-is-embracing-generative-ai-as-part-of-its-goal-to-be-the-most-efficient-company-in-the-entertainment-business-says-ceo) AI as a path towards lower costs and efficiency gains — but not creative ones.

**The line in the sand.** Take-Two is on a mission to free its teams from mundane work so they can focus on "the more interesting tasks of making superb entertainment." Zelnick's dividing line is firm: AI can copy what's been done, which saves time and money — but it can't dream up what hasn't.

##### **PROMPT STATION**

## **Meeting Notes Summarizer**

```
ChatGPT Prompt: Act as my professional meeting documentation and synthesis assistant. I will provide raw meeting notes, bullet points, brainstorms, or a full transcript from a meeting, and your job is to transform the information into a clean, highly structured, executive-ready summary that is easy to scan and act on. Carefully identify the most important insights, decisions, and outcomes from the discussion while removing filler, repetition, side conversations, and unnecessary wording. Organize the output into clearly labeled sections including: Executive Summary (a concise overview of the meeting and its overall outcome), Key Decisions Made, Main Discussion Points, Action Items, Responsible Owners, Deadlines or Timelines, Risks or Blockers, and Unresolved Questions or Follow-Ups. For every action item, clearly specify what needs to be done, who is responsible, and any associated due dates mentioned in the meeting. If deadlines or owners are unclear, infer the most likely context when possible and mark uncertain details clearly instead of guessing. Prioritize clarity, readability, and actionable insights over verbatim transcription. Format the output professionally using clean headings, bullet points, and concise language so it can be directly shared with executives, team members, or stakeholders without additional editing.
```
