AI Twitter Recap
Top Story: GLM 5.3 Flash launch and reactions
What happened
Z.ai formally launched GLM-5.3-Flash, revealing that the previously previewed “Ox Alpha” model is its public identity.
Z.ai announced GLM-5.3-Flash [ https://substack.com/redirect/56428322-1522-4e59-add1-689cf5ed9b1e?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] as a natively multimodal model with a 1M-token context window, 320B total parameters / 18B active parameters, released under the MIT License, and available via weights, API, chat, coding plan, and AutoClaw.
Z.ai simultaneously positioned it as a highly price-competitive successor to GLM-5.2, claiming on its internal benchmark that it outperforms GLM-5.2 at every effort level and is on par with Claude Opus 4.8 on coding [ https://substack.com/redirect/3a3de2bd-5bf2-4515-84cb-42084aa49ccf?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ].
The launch also resolved the long-running Ox Alpha mystery: multiple posters explicitly connected Ox Alpha to GLM-5.3-Flash, including SemiAnalysis [ https://substack.com/redirect/50222c15-bb60-4cbf-bec4-c7cf677e0c32?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], rasbt [ https://substack.com/redirect/cfc0fcb9-9cc0-434e-a0ac-1eb1454f42cc?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], theo [ https://substack.com/redirect/1824bc7a-8b71-4c26-bbc3-43ed9cc97275?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], and Cline [ https://substack.com/redirect/eff9f3a3-afe7-401e-b106-0ee2a77d42dd?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ].
Early third-party model infrastructure support appeared almost immediately: CoreWeave [ https://substack.com/redirect/45906b44-dd8f-42e4-879f-1d61214d3a75?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], Baseten [ https://substack.com/redirect/5936afef-dd07-4a29-b53a-e68a7bd81ce0?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], and Cline’s free integration in VS Code / JetBrains / CLI [ https://substack.com/redirect/c041d95a-4344-4f09-b21a-677a8b26263a?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ].
Shortly after launch, Z.ai engineer Zixuan Li said the chat template had been updated and early downloaders should re-download the model [ https://substack.com/redirect/88d1d218-cb09-40b7-afee-81a3449b50b0?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], implying a day-0 packaging or prompt-format correction.
Artificial Analysis first published an overview with an incorrect 400k context window, then issued a correction to 1M context [ https://substack.com/redirect/c06db2bd-3f7f-4aa0-b448-35ac4339766c?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], aligning with Z.ai’s original announcement.
Community response was unusually strong for an open-weight release, ranging from brief shock reactions like “HOLY” [ https://substack.com/redirect/38e0953d-1bd4-4d20-8e60-22ead5082039?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] to more substantive claims that the model may now be the best intelligence-per-dollar option, e.g. Artificial Analysis [ https://substack.com/redirect/8b725cb0-a5f7-45db-ac7f-525792d7b66c?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] and zainhas [ https://substack.com/redirect/29920a0b-ff56-4570-bea1-b7b686482115?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ].
The launch got folded into a broader narrative around Chinese frontier open models, with posts arguing that open Chinese labs are converging on similar architecture choices around linear attention, sparse attention, residual path design, and Muon [ https://substack.com/redirect/f4fed924-16ff-454b-9334-81d63371e4a1?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ].
Independent pushback emerged on at least one modality claim: skalskip92 [ https://substack.com/redirect/e389fb92-f655-4c3a-8666-4bc46658bc88?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] argued the model looks weak on several vision/object detection tasks despite being “native vision.”

Token-efficiency and reasoning mix
Artificial Analysis notes an interesting tradeoff:
GLM-5.3-Flash used 149M output tokens to run the Intelligence Index
compared with 168M for GLM-5.3
but more than Kimi K3 (133M) and Qwen3.8 2.4T A95B (136M) at similar Intelligence Index score
134M of the 149M tokens (~90%) were reasoning tokens
This is an important nuance: the model’s economics look excellent largely because token pricing is extremely low, not because it is especially token-frugal.

Architecture and systems details
Several technically informed reactions tried to reverse engineer or summarize what changed from GLM-5.2 / GLM-5.x.
The most detailed public architecture breakdown in the tweet set came from rasbt [ https://substack.com/redirect/cfc0fcb9-9cc0-434e-a0ac-1eb1454f42cc?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], who says GLM-5.3-Flash moves from GLM-5.2’s 744B-A40B backbone to 320B-A18B, and uses:
Kimi Linear-style 3:1 hybrid attention
34 KDA layers (Kimi Delta Attention)
11 MLA/DSA layers
MLA = Multi-head Latent Attention
DSA = DeepSeek Sparse Attention
DeepSeek V4-style mHC residual path
four parallel streams
plus a native vision encoder
The same tweet describes it as “super hybrid” because both major attention components are already “efficient” variants rather than a simple efficient/full-attention hybrid.
Another useful systems-oriented summary from thealexker [ https://substack.com/redirect/8b4b27a8-61cc-4878-8114-d4148bd4b1ee?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] frames the release as an efficiency story, highlighting:
compared to GLM-5.2:
~1/10 the cost
active params 32B → 18B
layers 92 → 45
hybrid linear + sparse attention
smaller average KV cache per layer
lower attention compute compounding at long contexts
claims that visual intelligence benefited from coding/RL style improvements
says the GLM-5.3 infrastructure agent co-authored parts of the work by helping with kernels, bottlenecks, and serving stack optimization
The broader context post from eliebakouch [ https://substack.com/redirect/f4fed924-16ff-454b-9334-81d63371e4a1?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] is opinionated but technically notable because it places GLM in a Chinese open-model trend:
nearly all Chinese frontier models now use linear attention
nearly all use sparse attention / indexer-compression designs
many use fancy residuals like mHC, attention residuals, gated residuals
many use Muon
That post is not a direct GLM paper summary, but it helps explain why the architecture details immediately resonated with model engineers: GLM-5.3-Flash appears to be another data point in a fast-converging efficiency-first Chinese frontier OSS design space.
Chinese chip angle and serving implications
The hardware/serving side was one of the most-discussed parts of the launch.
Z.ai itself said the model was “running entirely on Chinese AI chips” [ https://substack.com/redirect/56428322-1522-4e59-add1-689cf5ed9b1e?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]. The strongest amplification came from SemiAnalysis [ https://substack.com/redirect/50222c15-bb60-4cbf-bec4-c7cf677e0c32?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], which focused on the claim that 100T tokens/day are being served on Chinese chips. That tweet does not provide all the derivation, but it framed the infrastructure feat as the most shocking part of the reveal.
Reactions emphasized the significance:
theo [ https://substack.com/redirect/1824bc7a-8b71-4c26-bbc3-43ed9cc97275?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]: “Ox being a ‘flash’ model is insane. Serving all the traffic on Chinese chips is even more insane.”
same-day OSS mood post [ https://substack.com/redirect/818dd8b8-6930-46ed-89fc-fe868910f5a6?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] folded GLM into a broader celebratory open-source narrative.
There was also explicit back-of-envelope capacity reasoning from teortaxesTex [ https://substack.com/redirect/7ffbde26-8bbf-4c87-bff7-dc670a98cb51?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]:
If inference economics are comparable to V4-Flash,
10K tokens/s/NPU is “realistic”
864M/day per chip
100T/day would imply about 116K chips
suggesting 100K+ chips scale, “doable” but consuming an enormous fraction of total compute
That estimate is speculative rather than confirmed, but it shows how engineers interpreted the serving claim: not as marketing fluff alone, but as an infrastructure statement implying very large domestic accelerator fleets and mature inference optimization.
Adoption and distribution reactions
A notable part of the reaction cycle was how quickly usage posts appeared.
Cline [ https://substack.com/redirect/eff9f3a3-afe7-401e-b106-0ee2a77d42dd?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] said GLM-5.3 Flash was already its fastest growing model in Cline history, driving 11% of all traffic in less than a week, while also advertising it as free in Cline. Baseten [ https://substack.com/redirect/5936afef-dd07-4a29-b53a-e68a7bd81ce0?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]: day-0 availability, emphasizing general intelligence + agentic coding, native vision, and 1M context

This matters because it reinforces that GLM-5.3-Flash was not treated as a curiosity; it was immediately slotted into real inference/developer stacks.
