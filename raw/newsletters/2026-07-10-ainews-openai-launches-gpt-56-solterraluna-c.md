# [AINews] OpenAI launches GPT 5.6 Sol/Terra/Luna, Codex becomes ChatGPT
 superapp

On any other day, the launch of a surprisingly good/competitive Muse Spark 1.1 [ https://substack.com/redirect/dfbe2c59-e54e-4d9a-a298-2b5705c35603?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] from Meta Superintelligence Labs, including, for the first time, in the Meta Model API [ https://substack.com/redirect/8c956be8-67b0-435c-b36b-01e8474c71ed?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] (signaling high confidence for broad usage and third party testing which is bearing out in their sister models [ https://substack.com/redirect/eb043b86-7f07-4888-9313-6d4ed4a4ed5f?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]), would deserve title story status, but they had the misfortune of going up against a mainline frontier model launch:
As previewed a couple weeks ago [ https://substack.com/redirect/9f167e87-f816-4c4d-9488-78d8153db9e3?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] before government approval, 5.6 comes in three new sizes, Sol, Terra and Luna, corresponding to the sizes of Sun, Earth and Moon, as an alternative to the more literary sizing of Claude variants, and a new ultra effort level, “our highest-capability setting, coordinating multiple agents across parallel workstreams to finish complex tasks faster”:
max gives GPT‑5.6 even more time than xhigh to reason and explore alternatives, run checks, and revise its approach. ultra goes further by coordinating four agents in parallel by default, trading higher token use for stronger results and faster time-to-result on demanding tasks. 
On multiple benchmarks (not just the ones featured here), 5.6 both achieves higher performance at lower cost than Fable or Opus.
“Terra performs just above Fable 5, while Luna outperforms Opus 4.8; each does so in roughly one-third of the time, with about half as many output tokens, and at approximately one-quarter the estimated cost. It also sets new state-of-the-art results on Terminal‑Bench 2.1 and DeepSWE, which test complex command-line workflows and long-horizon engineering in real codebases.”
There are also harder-to-benchmark improvements in computer use, presentation/document generation, and scientific research that should nevertheless be taken very seriously.

AI Twitter Recap

OpenAI announced GPT‑5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna rolling out across ChatGPT, Codex, and the API via @OpenAI [ https://substack.com/redirect/f6525c3a-4fec-4f43-89d3-d795e2822796?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] and @OpenAIDevs [ https://substack.com/redirect/3113f436-ae18-4aed-8d03-24b79969c37f?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]

The “Sol autonomously post-trained Luna” claim
This was the most provocative technical claim around the launch, but its interpretation became contested almost immediately.
Multiple accounts amplified the statement that OpenAI says GPT‑5.6 Sol autonomously post-trained GPT‑5.6 Luna, via @scaling01 [ https://substack.com/redirect/9664db7c-2078-4853-b003-e4a400524243?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], @tejalpatwardhan [ https://substack.com/redirect/db0bcef1-ab3a-4e0f-a24b-f473e2c04b17?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], and @dejavucoder [ https://substack.com/redirect/6f21adde-0d84-4a93-b33c-77aae4f40a0a?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]
The claim fueled RSI/autoresearch speculation; @tenobrus [ https://substack.com/redirect/8e9a90c5-565c-41bd-a655-37d38665158d?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] said if true as stated, it would be a “pretty large update” for automated researcher timelines
@eliebakouch [ https://substack.com/redirect/2dfe24cd-e586-4056-8ddd-91904894b96b?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] framed it as OpenAI asking Sol to post-train Luna “with 100k GPUs” for an experiment
@gdb [ https://substack.com/redirect/d8e30466-47ed-40c0-8fd5-0d7e7fb14d01?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] said the implication is easy to overlook for accelerating engineering workflows, reinforcing that OpenAI wants this read as more than a marketing flourish
But skeptical clarifications emerged quickly: @nikolaj2030 [ https://substack.com/redirect/d21d2d69-abe1-4722-9b1c-6a9f2e83681e?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] asked whether this actually meant Sol completed a small controlled post-training task—modifying a config, editing a scheduler file, and launching a run—rather than end-to-end real-world post-training of Luna
