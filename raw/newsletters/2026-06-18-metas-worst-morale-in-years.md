## **Claude Fable’s plug is still pulled — and the situation has sent an unspoken message **


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/dbc51507-e8ec-469e-b19f-3acfc76c6a3d/superhumanteam_A_man_in_a_suit_pulling_a_robots_plug_out_of_a_0300aea1-b5ec-45cd-b17a-8ef059a030ee_1.jpg?t=1781729196)
Caption: Made with Midjourney


--------------------
**Pulling the plug.** Anthropic’s Claude Fable and Mythos went live for just days before the government suspended them, [citing](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access) national security. The two groups are [working](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/15/the-us-governments-anthropic-models-ban-was-never-about-an-ai-jailbreak/) on a solution, but the situation has sent a clear unspoken message — relying on a single model is becoming increasingly risky.

**Staying prepared. **As the industry evolves, it’s best to assume that access to your favorite model could shift without warning. Creating a handoff document can help you stay prepared to switch providers without slowing down. Just prompt your AI: "_Write a handoff document I can use to work successfully with another LLM. Include all stored memories, preferred workflows, and context you've learned._” Then feed that document to the next LLM to keep working smoothly.

**Avoiding model lock-in. **Want to go one step further? Consider downloading open-weight models, like Google’s [Gemma](https://deepmind.google/models/gemma/) family, to your own machine. Once downloaded, no lab or government can revoke your access. Other benefits: your data stays local, and ==you skip the monthly bills that come with frontier models.==

==**The bottom line. **====This is one of the first times that a government has pulled a live, public AI model== — but it likely won’t be the last. Keeping a handoff document ready or downloading open-weight models will make sure you don’t miss a beat as the landscape continues to change.

## **How to analyze data in Excel using ChatGPT**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/fc8aebb4-f355-4e08-bb7b-ce5e685655c0/image__3_.jpg?t=1781718796)

--------------------
* Open [Excel](https://www.microsoft.com/en/microsoft-365/excel) and click Add-ins in the Insert tab

* Search ‘ChatGPT’ > ‘Add’ to open it in the sidebar

* Click ‘Continue with ChatGPT’ > Login with your ChatGPT account

* Upload your data (Excel, CSV, image, PDF or anything) and start prompting

Sample Prompt: “give me a formula to calculate the total sales in column C for values above 500.”

* You can also prompt it to build financial models

* Ask questions, get formulas, or generate analysis in plain language, and it turns them into real, usable results
