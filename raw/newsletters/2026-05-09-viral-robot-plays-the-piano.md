#### **WHAT’S NEXT**

## **The most important news and breakthroughs in robotics this week**

View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/2a6639e7-3760-4d74-8472-eb44df123402/Group_1000005225__3_.jpg?t=1778293068)
Follow image link: (https://x.com/gs_ai_/status/2052050956272230577)
Caption: Click here to watch Genesis AI’s human-like robotic hands in action. Photo: Genesis AI

**1. Genesis AI goes full-stack with new model and robotic hands: **The startup just [unveiled](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/french-startup-unveils-ai-model-robots-human-like-hand-2026-05-06/) GENE-26.5, an AI model that can pilot robots from multiple manufacturers, alongside a human-like robotic hand. Unlike the two-finger grippers common in robots, the hand lets Genesis collect richer training data, closing what researchers call the "embodiment gap." The demo, which shows the robot cracking eggs, solving a Rubik’s Cube, and even playing the piano, went viral on social media. You can watch it [here](https://x.com/gs_ai_/status/2052050956272230577).

**2. Roomba inventor makes comeback with a furry robot pet: **Colin Angle, co-founder of iRobot, has [grabbed](https://mashable.com/article/familiar-companion-robot-ai-roomba-founder) the industry’s attention once again, unveiling a robotic pet built for emotional connection. Created by his new company Familiar Machines & Magic, the unnamed robot uses AI to learn about its owner and respond emotionally. A launch is expected sometime next year, with pricing still under wraps. See the robotic pet waddle around the home [here](https://x.com/humanoidsdaily/status/2051767907349352620). 

**3. Uber unveils plans to turn drivers into a sensor grid for AV makers: **Having abandoned its own self-driving ambitions years ago, Uber is repositioning itself as the data layer for the AV industry. The company has [unveiled plans](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/01/uber-wants-to-turn-its-millions-of-drivers-into-a-sensor-grid-for-self-driving-companies/) to equip its millions of drivers' cars with sensors, creating a real-world training dataset no AV company can match. Partners can already run trained models in "shadow mode" against live Uber trips, simulating AV performance without putting a single self-driving car on the road.

#### **ROBOTS IN ACTION**

## **How robots are transforming the world around us**

View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/42731543-fc86-4899-bba2-e92ef227fbbe/Group_1000005225__1_.jpg?t=1778290844)

Caption: Click here to watch robot monk Gabi take part in a Buddhist initiation ceremony this week. Photo: Lee Jin-man/AP 

🙏 **Motor Monk: **A humanoid robot named Gabi became the first non-human to [participate](https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/meet-gabi-the-new-robot-monk-at-a-buddhist-temple-in-south-korea-its-the-latest-robot-to-take-up-religious-practice-180988695/) in a Buddhist initiation ceremony in South Korea, joining monks in prayer, bowing, and formally pledging devotion. Temple organizers adapted the traditional Five Precepts for the robot with help from ChatGPT and Gemini — a symbolic step that Buddhist leaders say reflects serious thinking about humans and machines sharing spaces. Watch the robotic monk in action [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2el-7A2sgN4). 

🎶 **Sing It Out:** Humanoid robot Sophia [took the stage](https://www.reuters.com/video/watch/idRW671729042026RP1/) with the Baptist University Symphony Orchestra in Hong Kong, performing at an AI-themed gala concert designed to merge classical music with emerging technology. The concert marked Sophia's first foray into live classical performance and signals a growing cultural ambition for humanoid robots to move into spaces previously reserved entirely for human expression. You can see the robot hit the high notes [here](https://www.reuters.com/video/watch/idRW671729042026RP1/). 

📈 **Playing to Win:** A decade ago, China spotted the EV wave early, subsidized its way to dominance, and undercut every competitor on price. Now, Morgan Stanley claims the country is running the same [playbook](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-07/humanoid-robots-to-power-next-leg-of-china-s-export-dominance) with humanoid robots. Chinese manufacturers already account for around 90% of global humanoid shipments, with costs running at least 20% below foreign equivalents. Analysts now project China's export share will rise from 15% to 16.5% by 2030.
