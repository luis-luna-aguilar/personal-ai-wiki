## **Coinbase’s layoffs reveal the type of employee companies are looking for**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/9190a0d0-7792-4709-af88-1a1b2de8d316/superhumanteam_A_robot_wearing_2_hats_stacked_on_top_of_each__50811be9-c8d4-4fa8-901a-5cdbc0a3d538_3.jpg?t=1778023289)
Caption: Made with Midjourney


--------------------
**Widespread layoffs. **In an [open letter](https://x.com/brian_armstrong/status/2051616759145185723), Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong announced he’s cutting headcount by 14%, citing two reasons: crypto’s down market and AI. But similar to [Jack Dorsey’s letter](https://www.superhuman.ai/p/how-should-the-military-use-ai#:~:text=Block%20cuts%20its,last%20year%E2%80%99s%20numbers.) in March, this wasn’t typical PR talk — he broke down his specific reasoning behind the changes.

**Going lean.** Coinbase is flattening its org chart, making every manager a contributor, and refocusing around AI-native talent who can drive outsized impact using AI and agents. The shift reflects a broader [trend](https://x.com/gregisenberg/status/2051683558486392890) of prioritizing employees who can wear multiple hats.  

**Breaking the org chart. **Zencoder recently [praised](https://venturebeat.com/technology/when-product-managers-ship-code-ai-just-broke-the-software-org-chart) a product manager who used AI to build and ship a new feature — initiative that’s not in the job description but saved a week’s worth of meetings. Vercel has gone further by [hiring](https://cjroth.com/blog/2026-02-18-building-an-elite-engineering-culture) "design engineers" who can sketch and ship alone, collapsing two roles into one.

**The new operating model.** Coinbase, Zencoder, and Vercel are pointing in the same direction: smaller teams, fewer layers, and employees who leverage AI to punch above their weight. The age of the generalist has returned.
