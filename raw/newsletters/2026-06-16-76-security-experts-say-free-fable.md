## **Stanford's AI employment index shows one group is getting hit harder than others**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/e761b6c1-cd3a-4c03-aeb1-03712c08823a/superhumanteam_A_robot_teaching_in_front_of_a_declining_chart_6c6dbbc5-28d6-40ef-abdb-9ae533b1ad10_1.jpg?t=1781552510)
Caption: Made with Midjourney


--------------------
**AI’s impact.** Tech layoffs hit 40,000 in May, their highest single month in two years. While AI is often blamed as the cause, [some argue](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/ai-layoff-wave-becoming-powder-072541685.html#:~:text=Other%20voices%20have,Ah%2C%20it%27s%20AI.%22) it’s just a convenient excuse for overstaffed companies to shed headcount. Stanford’s Digital Economy Lab is trying to get clearer insight with [AI Economic Indicators](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/project/indicators/) — an evidence-based picture of how AI is actually reshaping work and productivity.

**Junior squeeze.** Stanford’s data — pulling from 25,000 firms — shows that AI hasn’t triggered a surge or collapse in overall hiring since ChatGPT was launched in 2022. But one demographic has had a different experience: early-career workers ages 22 to 25. For this group, employment in AI-exposed occupations has shrunk at 3.8% per year, while the least-exposed roles are growing at 2.0%.

**Feeling the impact.** The damage is concentrated in specific roles, with junior software developers and customer service workers seeing the most substantial declines, while less-exposed jobs like home health aides have actually added junior workers. 

**Hollow pipeline.** Stanford is upfront that these are early signals from a fixed sample — not a definitive picture of the global labor market. But if the pattern holds, and AI keeps hollowing out entry-level roles, the pipeline for senior talent will weaken right along with it.

## **How to turn any PDF into a Claude Skill**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/5fa14daa-f454-4757-9ab0-429d276fc744/Figma_Group_1000005287.jpg?t=1781554476)
Caption: 


--------------------
* Go to [Claude](https://claude.ai/new) and choose a PDF that you like to turn into a Claude Skill

* Extract the main system from the PDF: the steps in order, repeated rules, mistakes to avoid, key questions, and what a successful result looks like

* Open Claude and ask it to create a ‘SKILL.md’ using this prompt:

_“Use the skill-creator to build a skill from the uploaded PDF. Activate when I ask Claude to [exact task]. Core steps: [paste]. Rules: [paste]. Mistakes to avoid: [paste]. Questions used: [paste]. Success looks like: [paste]. Do not use for: [2–3 unrelated tasks]. Ask me anything missing before generating the SKILL.md, then score the skill out of 10.”_

* Test the skill with different prompts, unrelated requests, and incomplete inputs to ensure it activates correctly and asks for clarification when needed.

* Upload it in Claude through Settings → Capabilities → Skills → Upload

* Now use your slash command whenever you want Claude to apply the PDF method.
