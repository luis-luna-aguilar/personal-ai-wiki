AI Twitter Recap
Microsoft’s MAI-Thinking-1 Tech Report, Training Stack, and Frontier-Tuning Push
MAI-Thinking-1 is the day’s densest technical release: Microsoft introduced MAI-Thinking-1 [ https://substack.com/redirect/d862c445-74df-4de0-ba97-2534b2d1543a?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], a generalist/reasoning model trained without third-party distillation, reporting 97% on AIME 2025, 53% on SWE-Bench Pro, and human preference wins over Sonnet 4.6 in blind side-by-sides. The 109-page report was widely praised for unusual transparency by @eliebakouch [ https://substack.com/redirect/dbbb51bf-1861-4e52-a66c-2da60e26776d?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], @nrehiew_ [ https://substack.com/redirect/80bf287d-cba8-4d12-b40f-93c972a21396?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], and @mustafasuleyman [ https://substack.com/redirect/e8484f9e-a21f-47ad-8cf3-68889faa8a63?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]. The main technical theme: Microsoft appears to have “hillclimbed from scratch,” with @MinjiYoon90 [ https://substack.com/redirect/c363c5b1-9c8a-471f-a604-147ddd8b323e?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] explicitly framing the effort that way.
Why researchers cared about the report: The most-cited detail was not just benchmark quality, but the amount of systems/training information released. @eliebakouch [ https://substack.com/redirect/dbbb51bf-1861-4e52-a66c-2da60e26776d?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] highlighted zero synthetic data and zero prior-model distillation, meaning reasoning, tool use, and agentic behaviors were learned in post-training without a synthetic “cold start.” The thread also called out publication of the scaling ladder recipe, exact MFU numbers, and target-loss construction. In follow-ups, @eliebakouch [ https://substack.com/redirect/e6ee645c-24ba-4e33-bfc5-84164fdb2e7d?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] noted the private NLL mixture was weighted 50% code, 17.5% STEM, 17.5% math, 10% general knowledge, 5% multilingual, with normalization against an internal model; he also pointed out ablations around 100–200 TPP for their MoE setup here [ https://substack.com/redirect/ab63fd6f-0372-47d2-85b1-d15d5ecfd5a1?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]. Other notable implementation details surfaced in the community recap: Microsoft used SGLang in parts of the stack, per @eliebakouch [ https://substack.com/redirect/20bb6e03-1abb-495e-b12a-fdb63707114f?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], and dspy.GEPA for pretraining data curation, per @lateinteraction [ https://substack.com/redirect/e4d12281-0a18-4dbf-a03d-0f3ff999ca10?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] and @harold_matmul [ https://substack.com/redirect/d5e3e8a7-ef91-41a8-aa80-f97540d0281c?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ].
Microsoft’s productization angle goes beyond one model: Alongside the report, Microsoft pushed a broader “own your model” story. @mustafasuleyman [ https://substack.com/redirect/c08b7fbf-9763-42d2-b5e7-3f9628c7706b?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] outlined Frontier Tuning, centered on reinforcement-learning environments for workflow-specific adaptation, claiming internal Excel-oriented MAI-tuned models can reach GPT-5.4-level quality on relevant tasks while being up to 10× more efficient. The Build rollout also included MAI-Image-2.5 [ https://substack.com/redirect/62a9e968-5112-44bd-907b-a443c623de71?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], which Microsoft says is #3 on text-to-image and #2 on image-to-image arena leaderboards, plus MAI-Code-1-Flash [ https://substack.com/redirect/f0eab841-fe02-4044-9575-f7c8013c4060?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] and deployment into products like OneDrive Photos. As a meta-point, this is one of the clearest examples this year of a lab trying to publish a frontier-style report while simultaneously turning that stack into enterprise customization infrastructure.
Open Model Releases: Gemma 4 12B, Ideogram 4.0, Miso One, and Local-First Momentum
Gemma 4 12B was the standout open-model launch: Google released Gemma 4 12B [ https://substack.com/redirect/20934720-6f00-47e5-b3aa-201958325d3c?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], an Apache 2.0 multimodal model designed to run on-device with roughly 16GB VRAM. The architectural novelty is its encoder-free design: no separate vision or audio tower. As Google explained [ https://substack.com/redirect/13e9ded2-bfc7-4edc-9764-5b994b5f1f2a?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], images are handled via a lightweight embedding module and raw audio is projected directly into the text-token space. Community reaction focused on the elegance of collapsing modality encoders into the LLM backbone, with @googlegemma [ https://substack.com/redirect/db705a73-5404-4b5f-84ec-ba1e2f18d3d5?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], @googleaidevs [ https://substack.com/redirect/1688cc80-b98a-44bb-8862-341b437ffa75?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], @mtschannen [ https://substack.com/redirect/3b41406a-8f87-4a8f-9f04-49ee09109557?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], and @armandjoulin [ https://substack.com/redirect/cf8b22ba-844b-4622-b035-7105182853aa?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] all emphasizing the same point. Tooling support landed immediately across vLLM [ https://substack.com/redirect/9e135ba8-e8f6-41a1-8528-2371a79a6e28?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], Ollama [ https://substack.com/redirect/e8999c67-a448-4c1f-97ce-a106740607b2?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], llama.cpp/MLX via @osanseviero [ https://substack.com/redirect/9296e97b-080f-4575-aa6d-b68a7c8df926?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], and Unsloth GGUFs [ https://substack.com/redirect/a18cc1aa-637b-4131-82b3-455018963df3?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] that reportedly enable local runs with as little as 8GB RAM in quantized form.
Ideogram’s flip to open weights mattered as much as the model itself: Ideogram 4.0 [ https://substack.com/redirect/d466e927-5575-41cf-982d-af50049b4f5e?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] was announced as “the best open image model in the world,” with open weights and immediate deployment via fal [ https://substack.com/redirect/07e8b8db-28a5-4f36-9d1a-922d175c1ea7?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] and Hugging Face here [ https://substack.com/redirect/6649c1e9-5fd1-4e46-b95b-0f1bd5a93c7e?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]. Agents, Harnesses, and the Shift from Frameworks to Execution Layers
The center of gravity is moving from “frameworks” to agent harnesses and execution environments: Several posts converged on the same idea. @gakonst [ https://substack.com/redirect/12e16518-ac43-409d-a6ce-e82bc828dd8d?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] argued that the future IDE stack is less about code editors and more about replacing files with threads and bundling plan/design/build/deploy/monitor loops—leaving collaboration/sync engines as a key unsolved problem. In a complementary interview summary, @ConorBronsdon [ https://substack.com/redirect/e1bd2ce3-0dbd-4b7d-9144-b7b5646ce9d5?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] reported Jerry Liu’s view that the “framework era” is ending, with abstractions moving upward into skills, tools, and context quality rather than Python wrappers.
Multi-agent and agent-optimization work is getting more concrete: CMU/LTI’s MACU [ https://substack.com/redirect/992eec5d-4dd9-4293-8be7-32a829aa0063?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] and @kohjingyu’s thread [ https://substack.com/redirect/5b629f36-7534-48b9-a36f-81c544ae9245?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] argue that computer-use agents should be designed as multi-agent DAG-based systems, with a manager decomposing tasks and dispatching parallel subagents. Reported gains were 4.7–25.5% across benchmarks and 1.5× faster completion on Odysseys. On the optimization side, Microsoft’s SkillOpt got practical validation from @omarsar0 [ https://substack.com/redirect/7a6d84e1-db71-4d00-8b9a-c9966c12af4b?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], who says plugging it into an orchestrator improved one multimodal extraction skill from 0.73 to 0.93.
Agent UX and deployment tooling are becoming products in their own right: Nous’s Hermes Agent updates drew strong engagement, including remote-connection fixes here [ https://substack.com/redirect/29fb37d6-3f96-4182-87fa-d9e8f261208d?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], an updated remote guide here [ https://substack.com/redirect/2dfd32da-cdff-4356-93cc-6284a23f3cd1?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], and a larger dashboard overhaul here [ https://substack.com/redirect/65dbc95c-1c9f-40ba-a046-b93ed9cc0b93?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]. Perplexity launched Personal Computer for Windows [ https://substack.com/redirect/5b9efdc3-eb64-49f4-86c4-e15b7742e48e?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], an on-device orchestrator for apps/files, while Cloudflare Browser Run remote tabs [ https://substack.com/redirect/93eaeedc-010f-49dc-979d-e67ed738e469?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] showed a more agent-native browser control path. LangChain/LangSmith pushed on the observability and cost-control layer with Gateway spend tracking [ https://substack.com/redirect/1a47f198-6487-4714-989c-3ba3abf0a4ca?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], Sandbox/Gateway/Observability docs [ https://substack.com/redirect/c4cb1b50-bac2-46bd-9f77-f6a5f18441ba?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], and case studies around Deep Agents and LangSmith here [ https://substack.com/redirect/6d88e578-1ca4-4c2c-bd17-cefec83a7654?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ].
Routing, Cost Controls, and Open-vs-Frontier Deployment Strategy
Model routing is now a real debate, not a slogan: @levie [ https://substack.com/redirect/70a0d202-17ef-4729-9c75-0096eeb75ec4?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] argued that as token budgets become a meaningful opex category, model routing is inevitable, with domain-specific evals as the differentiator. But @scottastevenson [ https://substack.com/redirect/6db27141-bc8c-4b23-85b1-9747508b9b57?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] pushed back hard, calling most routing products “snake oil” so far: frontier models can be better/faster/cheaper in aggregate if they avoid retries; routing can destabilize tightly coupled systems; and API vendors can often internalize obvious arbitrage. @fabianstelzer [ https://substack.com/redirect/434ee212-edb0-4119-8fa3-df139be34ec3?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] added that cache writes and harness-model-prompt fit can erase expected savings.
Enterprise users are starting to enforce hard cost ceilings: @simonw [ https://substack.com/redirect/fb01973f-3ee5-4301-9d9f-4efe8faecf87?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] highlighted reports that Uber caps coding-agent spend at $1,500/month per employee per tool. LangChain immediately framed this as a use case for LangSmith Gateway [ https://substack.com/redirect/e96d4a18-7cfd-4332-b980-6e3e13b9ae44?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]. The broader sentiment was captured by @Yuchenj_UW [ https://substack.com/redirect/18c60514-ce03-493b-b233-ee336730676e?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]: some orgs may soon face a three-way choice between letting everyone “tokenmaxx,” capping budgets, or reducing headcount and reallocating spend to the most productive AI-enabled workers.
Real data points are starting to emerge for hybrid/open strategies: Harvey’s benchmark results were the cleanest example. In one study, Harvey [ https://substack.com/redirect/e6600fbc-73e5-4503-8662-04b28937b508?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ] found a hybrid legal agent with GLM 5.1 as the main worker and Opus 4.7 as an advisor beat pure Opus on all-pass rate (18% vs 14%) while costing $368 vs $954 across 100 tasks.
