## **In case you missed them: this week’s four biggest stories**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/fafbc43b-7d75-4568-a6b2-0077e624c6ed/superhumanteam_A_robot_sipping_coffee_and_reading_the_newspap_73ffb131-fed6-4481-9b53-49d413b387f1_1.jpg?t=1782398309)

--------------------
**OpenAI pressed its cyber advantage while Anthropic’s top models are on the bench.** Anthropic’s Mythos and Fable are still down as the AI leader negotiates with the US government to determine a safe path forward. OpenAI [seized the opening](https://openai.com/index/daybreak-securing-the-world/) this week by shipping its full GPT-5.5-Cyber to trusted defenders and launching [Patch the Planet](https://openai.com/index/daybreak-securing-the-world/), a program built to help organizations close vulnerabilities at scale.

**Anthropic dropped Claude into your Slack channels.** With [Claude Tag,](https://claude.com/product/tag) anyone on an Enterprise or Team plan can now tag @Claude in a Slack channel and have the AI jump in to help knock out work. It responds right in Slack, pulls context from the thread, and can work collaboratively with teammates. Top AI researcher Andrej Karpathy [called the update](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2069547676849557725) a “new paradigm” for using Claude, but others aren’t so sure.

**Agent “loops” are emerging as AI’s next big shift.** Claude Code creator Boris Cherny and OpenClaw founder Peter Steinberger — among others — both expect agent loops to be the next evolution of working with AI. Loops establish a repetitive workflow: you hand an agent a goal with clear instructions and success benchmarks, then let it execute, iterate, and check its own work. It swaps the back-and-forth conversation for an automated workflow. [Learn more in this viral article](https://x.com/AnatoliKopadze/status/2068328135611822149) (9M views).

**Sakana AI revealed a tool that goes toe-to-toe with Mythos and Fable.** [Fugu](https://sakana.ai/fugu-release/) is a platform that claims frontier-grade results by orchestrating multiple models under the hood, routing tasks to the optimal LLM. It runs through a single API endpoint and posts strong early benchmarks — though independent validation hasn't landed yet.

_Readers also loved _[_In The Weights_](https://intheweights.com/)_, a tool that tells you if you're important enough to be included in modern AI models' training data._

## **How to delegate work in Slack with Claude Tag**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/cc5a2329-d8d6-4221-94e4-dad6da911129/image__8_.jpg?t=1782394961)
Caption: Source: Claude


--------------------
* Open [Claude Tag](https://claude.com/product/tag), click Add to Slack, and complete the setup

* Grant Claude access to selected channels. Open the Slack channel where you want Claude to help

* Type **@Claude** followed by the task and give enough context

* Keep working while Claude researches, drafts, summarizes, or completes the task

* Review Claude's response, make any edits, and use the output however you need

**_Example prompt_**_: @Claude Review the last week's product discussion in this channel. Summarize the key decisions, list any open action items, identify blockers, and draft a Slack update I can share with leadership._

Pro tip: Treat Claude like a coworker, not a chatbot. Assign complete tasks with clear outcomes, and include any context or tool access it needs to finish the job.
