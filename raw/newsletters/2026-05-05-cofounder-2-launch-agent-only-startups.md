## **A senior researcher at DeepMind argues AI will never be conscious, even if we achieve AGI**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/5c61f82b-70b4-415e-bad6-dd1d488570af/superhumanteam_A_robot_holding_a_lightbulb_thats_dark_--ar_16_ba68c112-26ed-43d2-bfdd-9b02d7427739_2.jpg?t=1777923898)
Caption: Made with Midjourney


--------------------
**The Abstraction Fallacy. **Google DeepMind’s Alexander Lerchner believes AI will never achieve consciousness. Not in 5 years, not in 500. His recent paper, [The Abstraction Fallacy](https://deepmind.google/research/publications/231971/), argues that subjective experience can't emerge from symbolic computation — and no amount of training data will change that.

**The core argument.** Lerchner drills into the difference between instantiation and simulation. A human brain instantiates consciousness, where the physical process itself _is_ the experience. AI _simulates_ it, producing outputs that mimic consciousness without ever generating the thing itself.

**The viral line.** One passage from the paper racked up 1M+ views on X. Lerchner [writes](https://x.com/Hesamation/status/2045353213436567694): “Expecting an algorithmic description to instantiate the quality it maps is like expecting the mathematical formula of gravity to physically exert weight." In other words, an algorithm describing an emotion is still just a description — the way a map of Manhattan isn't actually Manhattan.

**The consciousness debate. **Some leaders have speculated that today’s models could be conscious, with Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei [saying](https://www.newsweek.com/anthropic-ceo-raises-unsettling-possibility-about-ai-11644833#:~:text=%22We%20are%20not%20even%20sure%20that%20we%20know%20what%20it%20would%20mean%20for%20a%20model%20to%20be%20conscious%2C%20or%20whether%20a%20model%20can%20be%20conscious.%20But%20we%27re%20open%20to%20the%20idea%20that%20it%20could%20be.%22) he’s “open to the idea that it could be.” Lerchner argues that simulation, no matter how sophisticated, never crosses into experience.

##### **PROMPT STATION**

## **Social Media Growth**

```
Prompt: Act as a world-class social media growth strategist. I run a brand in [industry] targeting [describe your audience in detail: demographics, goals, pain points, behavior], and my primary platform is [platform]. Build a high-performance growth strategy designed to maximize reach, trust, and monetization simultaneously.

Break down the strategy into clear components: content pillars that align with audience psychology and platform algorithms, specific content formats that drive virality and saves/shares, and a posting cadence optimized for consistency and compounding growth. Include tactical engagement strategies (comment hooks, DM flows, audience interaction loops), and outline how to build a loyal community rather than just an audience.

Also design clear conversion pathways from content to lead capture to revenue while ensuring the content still feels native and not overly sales-driven. Prioritize actionable, non-generic insights, and structure the strategy so it can be directly implemented.
```
