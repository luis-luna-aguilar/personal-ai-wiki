**From:** AINews <swyx+ainews@substack.com>

View this post on the web at https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-gemini-4-argon-gdms-answer

GDM last shipped a larger-than-Flash model in February (3.1 Pro [ https://substack.com/redirect/7f57bf60-b33f-4e04-b4fe-100cd85cdd96?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]), and after successive incremental 3.x Flash versions and the big GDM management shakeup  [ https://substack.com/redirect/fa23424b-1e28-4aec-80b7-bf7c359bc5b5?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]last month, the largest question for GDM was when they would catch up to peers who have in the meantime launched Fable and Astra class models. 
Well, Argon’s here [ https://substack.com/redirect/e0e77f80-4525-4674-98c7-285d4729b5a2?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], with VERY respectable benchmarks (SOTA in 13 of 19 credible benchmarks)… but only accessible in limited cybersecurity preview, though access is promised “as soon as possible [ https://substack.com/redirect/019a3075-6407-440d-a853-214a3fb57099?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ]”:
We like the experimental Long Decode Continuation [ https://substack.com/redirect/7d568e14-ac44-4c9c-aff5-fbe4ccc543dc?j=eyJ1IjoiODlhdDlmIn0.psR-epQqK_Wbg6RTI-yQkSgRmDIqosMhU9elL-EoqO0 ], which increases output tokens up to 1M as an industry first.
