## **Agent loops could become AI’s defining trend for the rest of 2026**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/b246d13e-92aa-4089-89a5-474cb1cd9e62/superhumanteam_A_robot_holding_a_hula_hoop_overhead_--ar_21_-_1e5bc409-b41c-40f6-ae83-91cd5dce2eba_2.jpg?t=1782179872)
Caption: Made with Midjourney


--------------------
**In agreement.** Two of AI’s leading voices, Claude Code [creator](https://x.com/bcherny/status/2063792263067754658) Boris Cherny and OpenClaw [founder](https://x.com/steipete/status/2063697162748260627) Peter Steinberger, have agreed on what AI’s next biggest trend will likely be: loops. This productivity strategy involves creating a repetitive workflow (or loop) for your AI, so it can work efficiently — even when you’re not telling it to.

**What’s looping? **Looping is an emerging way to work with AI agents. Instead of chatting to your AI like a coworker, you act as the manager and treat it like your employee. You establish a goal with clear instructions and benchmarks for success. It then executes a plan, iterates, and verifies its answers. Once the loop is built, the agent can run it repeatedly without constant back-and-forth — a massive unlock for productivity.

**The critical piece.** What makes looping work is the verification step. By defining what success looks like for your agent, you can require it to double-check its own work to ensure quality. Without this step, a looping agent could churn out unusable results while burning through your usage limits.

**Emerging sector.** Looping requires technical know-how and can also be prohibitively expensive, so it likely isn’t a good fit for every task — but expect this concept to grow in popularity over the coming months. Want to learn more? This viral essay breaks down [everything you need to know about loops](https://x.com/AnatoliKopadze/status/2068328135611822149) (7M views).

## How to fill out forms by talking to ChatGPT


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/3aaa6b66-042a-470e-95fc-002fab21d848/Figma_Group_1000005300__2_.jpg?t=1782135824)
Caption: 


--------------------
* Open [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/) on mobile and start voice mode

* Upload or take a photo of the form and tell ChatGPT what information goes where

_**Sample Prompt: **__“Look at this form and fill it out using the details I give you. Ask me if any required field is missing. Keep the formatting clean and don’t add anything I didn’t say.”_

* Talk through the details field by field

* Ask ChatGPT to return a completed version you can review

* Double-check names, numbers, dates, addresses, and signatures before submitting

Exercise caution when sharing sensitive information like passwords, banking details, government IDs, medical records, company data, and so on.

##### **PROMPT STATION**

## Idea to Product Roadmap

```
Claude Code Prompt: "I'm starting a new project. I'll explain what I want to build and its features in my own words after this. Before proposing any plan, ask me questions about the project — keep asking, one topic at a time, until you fully understand what we're building and what the end product should look like 100%. Don't move forward on assumptions or guesses, even small ones. Once you have a complete picture, write down the full project vision in a clear, AI-readable format — detailed enough that a fresh session could read it and understand the project fully without me re-explaining anything. Then walk me through the key technical decisions one at a time (frontend, backend, database, hosting, auth, etc.), explain each choice in plain language, and clearly flag anything that would be costly or hard to undo later. Only after we've agreed on the overall structure, propose a build plan broken into small, reviewable stages — not the whole thing at once. Also tell me along the way if any tools, connectors, or skills would help specifically for this project, and how I'd connect them."
```
