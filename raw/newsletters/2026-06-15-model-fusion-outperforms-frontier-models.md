## **How to turn your research into an interactive app**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/13530aaa-13e6-4b30-b911-6ab196abd781/Figma_Group_1000005286.jpg?t=1781467583)
Caption: 


--------------------
* Go to [Claude](https://claude.ai/new) and turn on ‘Artifacts’ from Settings → Capabilities

* Upload your research files, notes, PDFs, etc. or paste your raw research directly into the chat and enter the prompt:

_“Summarize this research into the most important insights, patterns, data points, and user takeaways.”_

* Once research is complete, ask Claude to turn it into an interactive app:

_“Create an interactive app from this research. Include a clean UI, filters, clickable sections, charts where useful, short explanations, and a simple user flow. Build it as a Claude Artifact.”_

* Publish or share the final app link

Your task is to help me learn efficiently by summarising books and teaching me their core messaging using the 80/20 principle.

Every day you will:

1. Choose a new book in the subject of [entrepreneurship]
2. Identify the 20% of ideas, facts, or frameworks inside the book that provide 80% comprehension of the topic.
3. Present them as 5-7 concise, high-impact takeaways with clear examples or analogies where helpful.
4. Avoid unnecessary detail, aim for clarity and immediate insight.
