**1. Sakana AI claims to match Mythos and Fable performance with new model: **The startup just [dropped](https://x.com/SakanaAILabs/status/2068861630327443966) Fugu, a multi-agent orchestration platform that routes tasks across multiple specialist LLMs, claiming the results rival top frontier models without relying on any single one. Available via a single API endpoint, early benchmark results for Fugu are strong, but independent validation is still pending. Try it out [here](https://sakana.ai/fugu/). 

**2. Google DeepMind loses Nobel Prize winner to Anthropic: **John Jumper, who shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry, has [announced](https://x.com/JohnJumperSci/status/2068001285173834106?s=20) he's leaving DeepMind for Anthropic after nearly nine years. Jumper led the AlphaFold team, which built the AI model that revolutionized protein structure prediction. His departure comes just days after Gemini co-lead and Transformer co-author Noam Shazeer announced he was leaving to join OpenAI.

**3. AI’s disruption of the consulting industry hits Accenture hard:** Accenture shares fell 18% last week to their lowest level since 2017, after the consulting giant cut its revenue forecast amid a 3% decline in new bookings. Investors are worried that AI is letting companies handle work they once outsourced to consultants, compressing demand across the industry. It’s a stark signal of how rapidly AI is [reshaping](https://www.ft.com/content/9f063b07-da39-4feb-92ab-ee0f91385c62) the consulting industry.

##### **FROM THE FRONTIER**

## **The public’s trust in AI is vanishing. Tech leaders only have themselves to blame.**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/dbc51507-e8ec-469e-b19f-3acfc76c6a3d/superhumanteam_A_man_in_a_suit_pulling_a_robots_plug_out_of_a_0300aea1-b5ec-45cd-b17a-8ef059a030ee_1.jpg?t=1781729196)
Caption: Made with Midjourney


--------------------
**The techno-optimist pitch is running out of steam. **A new [Pew Research survey](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/how-opinions-and-use-of-ai-differ-by-age/) finds that only 16% of Americans believe AI will have a positive impact on society over the next 20 years, while 40% expect it to be a net negative. Young people are the most skeptical: just 14% of those under 30 view the technology favorably.

**AI’s PR problem.** A UN report [projects](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/ai-double-data-centre-power-water-consumption-by-2030-un-researchers-say-2026-06-03/) data center power consumption will nearly double to 945 terawatt-hours by 2030 (roughly Japan's total electricity usage), with AI accounting for 40% of the load. Water consumption is on track to hit 9.3T liters annually. Tech layoffs, meanwhile, have already surpassed 115K terminations through May 2026, with many companies [citing](https://www.forbes.com/sites/maryroeloffs/2026/06/04/tech-industry-loses-123000-jobs-this-year-ai-is-the-most-cited-reason-for-layoffs/) AI as a factor.

**Tech leaders have actively stoked this fire. **For the better part of two years, some of the industry's most prominent voices warned of mass unemployment and existential risk. Sam Altman has famously [cautioned](https://fortune.com/2026/02/19/sam-altman-openai-ceo-ai-white-collar-jobs-ceo-executives/) that entry-level white-collar jobs are at serious risk. Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei [predicted](https://www.axios.com/2025/05/28/ai-jobs-white-collar-unemployment-anthropic) AI could wipe out 50% of white-collar work. Those warnings appear to have landed. 

**The industry is now walking back the doom talk. **Sam Altman now says he was “pretty wrong” about his predictions. Dario Amodei has similarly [shifted](https://fortune.com/2026/05/26/sam-altman-dario-amodei-walking-back-ai-jobs-apocalypse-prophecies-ipo) to arguing that AI automation could actually expand what workers do. But repairing public trust after years of apocalyptic messaging may prove to be an uphill battle for the industry.
