Gemini gets direct file generation for Workspace and Microsoft 365**: Gemini can now [create](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/generate-files-in-gemini/) Docs, Sheets, Slides, Word docs, Excel files, and more directly in the chat. Just prompt Gemini with your task and download the file straight to your device — eliminating the need to copy/paste. Supported formats include .pdf, .docx, .xlsx, .csv, LaTeX, TXT, RTF, and Markdown. [See how it works.](https://x.com/sundarpichai/status/2049519281600373159)

**2. Apple’s iOS 27 to bring AI deeper into the Photos app:** Apple's upcoming OS will reportedly [add](https://www.engadget.com/ai/ios-27-will-reportedly-come-with-new-ai-powered-photo-editing-tools-194119562.html) three new Apple Intelligence photo tools: Extend to expand backgrounds with generative AI, Enhance for auto lighting and quality fixes, and Reframe to shift perspective on spatial photos. Apple will likely unveil these new features at WWDC on June 8.

**3. Parag Agrawal's agent-search startup hits $2B valuation**: [Parallel Web Systems](https://parallel.ai/), founded by former Twitter CEO Parag Agrawal, just [raised](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/29/parallel-web-systems-hits-2b-valuation-five-months-after-its-last-big-raise/) $100M at a $2B valuation — nearly tripling its valuation from $740M just five months ago. The startup offers a suite of internet and research APIs optimized for AI agents, letting them search, extract, and monitor the web with the precision long-running tasks demand.

## **Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, and Microsoft flex their AI muscles**


--------------------
View image: (https://media.beehiiv.com/cdn-cgi/image/fit=scale-down,format=auto,onerror=redirect,quality=80/uploads/asset/file/24eb96bd-f116-4458-b433-73ad2e4538e7/superhumanteam_Four_robots_lifting_weights_in_the_gym_--ar_16_8ab5dec4-a513-4fc4-901e-f93609b99aac_3.jpg?t=1777501147)
Caption: Made with Midjourney


--------------------
**Big Tech delivers.** In a rapid-fire window, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, and Microsoft gave updates almost simultaneously. The consensus: Cloud revenue is soaring and AI-linked income is finally starting to materialize. The catch — capital expenditures are still climbing fast.

**Alphabet flexes. **The search giant [posted](https://s206.q4cdn.com/479360582/files/doc_financials/2026/q1/2026q1-alphabet-earnings-release.pdf) $109B in revenue, up 22% YoY, its fastest growth since 2022. Its main headline was Google Cloud, which grew 63% to $20B and now carries a $460B backlog. CEO Sundar Pichai made it clear: “Our AI investments and full stack approach are lighting up every part of the business.”

**AWS wins.** Amazon [announced](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/news-release/news-release-details/2026/Amazon-com-Announces-First-Quarter-Results/default.aspx) $181B in revenue with 28% AWS growth — the cloud unit's fastest in 15 quarters. Andy Jassy noted that Amazon's chips business cleared a $20B run rate growing triple digits, and Bedrock processed more tokens in Q1 alone than in all prior years combined. Q1 capital expenditures hit $44B, up from $25B a year ago.

**Meta beats. **Mark Zuckerberg's ad machine [delivered](http://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2026/Meta-Reports-First-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx) $56B in revenue — up 33% YoY, the company's fastest growth since 2021. Meta also raised 2026 capex guidance to a high of $145B, citing higher component pricing and data center costs. 

**Copilot lifts off.** Microsoft [hit](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/Investor/earnings/FY-2026-Q3/press-release-webcast) $82B in revenue, up 18% YoY, primarily driven by Azure growth. Microsoft 365 Copilot crossed 20 million paid seats, up from 15 million just a quarter ago, and the company’s total AI annualized revenue now stands at $37B, up 123% YoY.
