# Vibe Check: GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol Leave Us Wanting More

GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol handle bounded tasks efficiently. The work we most want help with still demands more judgment and follow-through.

November 17, 2026

At the start of this year, **[Mike Taylor](/@mike_2114?utm_cta_source=post_4621_main_content_mike_taylor_1)**, Every’s head of evals, was testing AI models on a set of tasks that none of them could do well: a training curriculum built around a theme, a minute-by-minute plan for running a show, and exercises for executives, each with its own prompt and realistic data. OpenAI’s two new models, **GPT-6.2 Astra** and **GPT-6.2 Sol**, handle all of them. Their predecessors already did well on these tasks, too.

“These tasks were all basically unsolved at the beginning of the year... now Codex has kinda saturated them,” Mike says. “Which is great to see. But leaves me wanting more.”
