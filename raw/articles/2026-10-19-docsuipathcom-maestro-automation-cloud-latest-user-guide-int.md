Maestro capabilities for orchestrating automation, AI agents, and human work through Business Process Model and Notation (BPMN), Flow, and Case Management.

What is Maestro?​
link

UiPath Maestro™ is a cloud-native orchestration platform that unifies automation, AI agents, and human interactions into streamlined, end-to-end business processes.

Maestro supports three complementary modeling approaches:

Business Process Model and Notation (BPMN) for standards-based structured process diagrams.
Flow for developer-first structured and sequential workflows.
Case Management for long-running, exception-heavy work that cannot be fully defined upfront.

BPMN and Flow run on the same Maestro engine. All three approaches share the same UiPath platform services and can work together, so a case can invoke a BPMN process as one of its task types. To choose the right approach, see Choosing a Maestro modeler and When to use BPMN Process vs. Case Management.

Maestro also supports defining business rules with Decision Model and Notation (DMN), and coordinates multiple actors—including RPA bots, AI tools, and people—within a single process.

This approach replaces disconnected automation tasks with intelligent, governed workflows that are easier to manage, adapt, and scale.

Enterprise capabilities​
link

Designed for enterprise use, Maestro supports complex, long-running workflows with:

Real-time monitoring and centralized control
Live instance supervision, including pause, resume, and retry
Detailed analytics and continuous optimization through process mining
Built-in compliance tools for governed orchestration

By providing a single platform to orchestrate agentic workflows, Maestro helps organizations improve operational efficiency, reduce manual work, and accelerate digital transformation.

Maestro serves as a mechanism to enhance the performance of your automated workforce and systems. It combines process data with task and automation-level data to better understand your processes.
