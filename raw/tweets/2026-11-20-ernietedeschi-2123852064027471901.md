The agentic story on the eve of the 2026 holiday season
1 min read

At @stripe, we’ve been following consumer agentic commerce as we get closer to the first holiday season with agents shopping. Here’s an overview of what we’re seeing so far:

Agentic payments continue to grow fast. Daily spend on @Link Agent Wallet—which powers payments for personal agents including Muse, Instinct, and Grok—was nearly 80-fold higher in mid-November than at the start of September, measured using a seven-day rolling average. In the last month alone, we’ve seen spend double.
Airfare and retail are currently the dominant spend categories. Last week, over 40% of Link agentic spending volume was on airfare, travel, and lodging, roughly a third on retail, and a quarter on other categories.
Agentic is sticky. More than half of consumers who make one agentic purchase come back to make at least a second, typically in the same spending category.

We’ll be monitoring the data as we get closer to Black Friday/Cyber Monday and plan to share more updates!
