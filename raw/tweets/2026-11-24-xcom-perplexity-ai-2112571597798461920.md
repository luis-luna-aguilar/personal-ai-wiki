# Perplexity on X — Perplexity Computer runs fully local on Mac

**@perplexity_ai · 9:48 AM · Oct 20, 2026 · 401.8K views**

Perplexity Computer can now run fully local on your Mac.

Work with your native Mac apps and local files, and run tasks without sending sensitive data to the cloud. The model and the agent both run on your Mac.

Available now in the Perplexity Mac app.

**@perplexity_ai · Oct 20 (reply)**

Local tasks use PPLX Pro, our post-trained version of Qwen 3.8, and don't consume Computer credits.

For more complex work, Hybrid can split the task between a cloud models and a local model on your Mac, keeping sensitive data private.

Read more: "Perplexity Computer runs fully local on Mac" (perplexity.ai)

**@perplexity_ai · Oct 20 (reply)**

Available now to Pro, Max, and Enterprise subscribers on supported Apple silicon Macs. 36 GB of unified memory is recommended.

Download the Perplexity Mac app and set up a local model to get started. ("Run Computer locally on your Mac | Perplexity", perplexity.ai)
