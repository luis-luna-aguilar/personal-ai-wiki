Chris
@ChrisGPT
It’s funny how few people seem to understand that white-collar labor probably has 3 years left before most of its usefulness gets completely abstracted away.

There will be a thin layer of hyper niche individuals with extreme domain knowledge and, taste, and their job will be maneuvering vast sea of autonomous agents toward the their will.

But alas, nobody seems to care. Simultaneously, we all acknowledge artificial superintelligence is on the horizon for the early 2030s, and yet few conversations for the impending massive unemployment, outside of a bi-annual essay from Dario Amodei.

It's certainly not going to be boring watching all the politicians begin to scramble in 2029 when they realize that massive unemployment is imminent. And I'm sure nothing will go wrong, considering we're all going to wait till the last possible minute before discussing economic policy.
