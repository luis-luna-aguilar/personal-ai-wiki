# Neal Patel (@neal_k_patel) — Introducing Constrained Decoding Models (CDMs)

Neal Patel
@neal_k_patel
You already gave the LLM the answer. So why is it writing it out from scratch?

Introducing a new AI model architecture: Constrained Decoding Models (CDMs)

LLMs were built to write. Most production workloads just need an answer: fast, consistent, and affordable.

CDMs are purpose-built for 1 task each. They read your input once and skip the token-by-token generative loop that makes LLMs slow and expensive.

Not just a decision model, but a family of 3 CDMs live today:

• Classify: define your labels and get back a calibrated probability + reason for each. Used for routing, tagging, and intent detection.
 • Extract: describe the entities in natural language and get back well-formed JSON with confidence scores + citations. Used for web scraping, document processing, and PII redaction.
 • Summarize: pass in long text, images, or PDFs and get back a consistent summary under 1,000 tokens. Used for context compaction, metadata generation, and transcript digests.

Built for production:

• 1M tokens of multimodal (text + image) input, 31x larger than Jev
 • 77.2% on DecisionBench vs Jev's 72%
 • 200x faster, 467x cheaper than frontier LLM APIs
 • $0.05/M input tokens. Output is free (yes, even for summarize)
