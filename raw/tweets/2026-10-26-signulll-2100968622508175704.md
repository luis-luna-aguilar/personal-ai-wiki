browser use is a commodity tool. & at some point it’ll be quite useless for two reasons (top websites if they care to survive will need to introduce agent tools directly & the web is extremely top heavy, 98% of traffic goes to ~100 domains).

there is no secret sauce to logging into a website to complete a task & no durable moat in our agent can open chrome, log in, click buttons, & submit a form.

i have written about this a while now (you can search my posts).
